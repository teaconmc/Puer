# Modular Golems

Modular Golems is a mod by lcy0x1 (`dev.xkmc`, LGPL-2.1) that turns standard golems into modular, upgradeable companions. Version `26.1.2.0+11+dungeon` targets Minecraft 26.1.2 with NeoForge 26.1.2.42-beta and uses the mod id `modulargolems`.

## What this mod adds

- **Golem entity types** — `modulargolems:metal_golem`, `modulargolems:humanoid_golem`, `modulargolems:dog_golem` plus the `modulargolems:beacon_laser` projectile.
- **Modular golem parts** — body/arms/legs (and left/right/leg for the metal golem) crafted from a `metal_golem_template` via the Stonecutter.
- **Materials** — data-driven golem materials from the generated `modulargolems:vanilla` config: copper, iron, gold, netherite and sculk (third-party material integrations are disabled on this branch).
- **Upgrades and modifiers** — numerous `modifier.modulargolems.*` upgrades (attack, regen, slots, recycle, sponge, swim, ...) applied to golems; the `max` advancement requires 12 upgrades.
- **Golem workbench** — `modulargolems:golem_workbench` block for assembling golems.
- **Command, summon and retrieval wands** — tools for ordering, summoning and collecting golems.
- **Several custom attributes** — `modulargolems:golem_regen`, `golem_sweep`, `golem_size`, `golem_jump`, `dynamic_reduction`.
- **Advancement tree** — a full `advancements.modulargolems.golems` line from first golem to an army.
