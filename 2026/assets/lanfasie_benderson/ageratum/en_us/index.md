# Lanfasie: Chapter Benderson

**Lanfasie: Chapter Benderson** (namespace `lanfasie_benderson`, version `0.1.1`, MIT) is a boss-fight mod for Minecraft 26.1.2 on NeoForge. It adds a single scripted arena encounter — **Benderson the Dawnwaiter of the Fallen Abyss** — plus the weapons, curses, adds and arena blocks that the fight is built from.

The mod has **no crafting recipes at all**. Everything is obtained from the creative tab `lanfasie_benderson:main` ("Lanfasie: Chapter Benderson") or from the fight itself.
