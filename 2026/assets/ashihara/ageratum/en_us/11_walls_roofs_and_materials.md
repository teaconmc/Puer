# Walls, Roofs, and Materials

Building items form large shape and material families: beams and frames, wall skins and rammed earth, flat and round tiles, eaves, ridges, corners, and metal ornaments. Ratios and directions in item names distinguish lengths, slopes, and edge variants. Compare members of one family in the Creative inventory before choosing them.

## Organizing a large project

- Build a full-scale sample for each roof pitch and record its main tile, half piece, oblique tile, and edge combination.
- Lay a continuous wall base before adding windows, framing, and metal joints.
- Avoid packing too many overlapping components into one composite block. Removal depends on hitting the intended part, and occlusion makes later editing harder.
- Material upgrading follows priority rather than free two-way replacement. Preserve original material and a project backup.

Many roof and wall components have no crafting recipes, so the current collection behaves primarily as a Creative building library. A survival building server should add stonecutting, a quest shop, or standardized material crates instead of making players investigate hundreds of absent recipes.
