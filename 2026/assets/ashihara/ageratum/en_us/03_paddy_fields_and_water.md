# Paddy Fields and Water

<recipe id="ashihara:iron_wide_hoe"/>

The Iron Wide Hoe performs different operations according to the target and whether the player is sneaking:

- Normal use on tillable soil creates a Dirt Depression and drops `2` Dirt Balls.
- Using it on the depression again converts that block into a Water Field.
- Using it on sand creates a Salt Field.
- Sneak-use converts a `3x3` area around the target into vanilla farmland and costs `3` durability.

<block id="ashihara:dirt_depression"/>

<block id="ashihara:water_field"/>

## Maintaining water level

After a seedling is planted, the Water Field level must be greater than `5` for rice growth. Complete the connected field and water supply before planting so isolated low-level plots do not stall. If rice remains unchanged for a long time, inspect the field state under it before adding light or simply waiting longer.

::: tip
The sneaking action creates vanilla farmland, not Water Field. It is useful for a seedling nursery, but final transplant plots still need the individual Dirt Depression -> Water Field conversion.
:::
