# Harvest and Rice Processing

Mature paddy-field rice drops Rice Crop. Place it on Rice Drying Sticks and wait for dry weather without rain to obtain Dried Rice Crop.

<block id="ashihara:rice_drying_sticks"/>

<recipe id="ashihara:rice_drying_sticks"/>

::: warning
Dried Rice Crop currently has no recipe or processing use. The paddy-field route ends here and cannot produce Brown Rice, Rice, or cooked food.
:::

## Completable Wild Rice route

Paddy dropped directly by Wild Rice can be crafted into a Paddy Pile and then processed in a Mortar. Ordinary crafting also splits a pile back into Paddy for batch adjustment.

<recipe id="ashihara:polymerize_paddy_pile"/>

<recipe id="ashihara:depolymerize_paddy"/>

Use the required pestle actions on a Paddy Pile to obtain Brown Rice, then process it further into Rice. Current normal rice grinding requires `6` pestle actions per batch. Rice can be cooked in a Pot or used by standard recipes such as Onigiri and Sushi.
