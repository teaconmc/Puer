# Wood, Lighting, and World Generation

Ashihara provides cherry, red wood, and maple families with logs, planks, stairs, slabs, fences, and buttons. Cherry Planks can be made from cherry logs. They are useful for establishing the ordinary building volume before miniature components add eaves and joints.

<recipe id="ashihara:cherry_planks"/>

Great cherry trees are configured for forest-tagged biomes, while Wild Rice enters plains with rarity `30`. A modpack may alter biome tags or remove features through data packs, so investigate forests and plains separately instead of treating both natural entries as one generation condition.

<block id="ashihara:jinja_lantern"/>

<recipe id="ashihara:jinja_lantern"/>

Lighting includes Jinja Lanterns, Stone Lanterns, long lanterns, hanging lamps, candlesticks, and oil plates at several scales. Some double-height lamps require open space above. Clear the whole footprint before placement rather than checking only the floor block.

::: note
Natural generation supplies only the Wild Rice and intended great-cherry entries. It does not automatically provide every miniature component, furniture item, or other crop seed.
:::
