# Wild Rice and Seedlings

<block id="ashihara:wild_rice"/>

Wild Rice is added to plains-type biomes with a rarity parameter of `30`. Harvesting it produces Paddy, the most reliable current entry into rice processing. Preserve part of the first harvest for propagation; the remainder can enter the paddy-pile and Mortar chain directly.

## Obtaining seedlings

Plant Paddy on ordinary farmland to create an immature rice plant. Let that plant mature and harvest it to obtain Rice Seedlings for a paddy field. Seedlings belong on Water Field blocks rather than vanilla farmland, and the field must maintain enough water for continued growth.

Do not confuse the two current routes:

- **Completable food route:** Wild Rice -> Paddy -> Paddy Pile -> Mortar processing -> Brown Rice and Rice.
- **Paddy-field showcase route:** Paddy -> nursery farmland -> Rice Seedling -> paddy-field rice -> Rice Crop -> Dried Rice Crop.

The second route currently stops after drying; no recipe turns Dried Rice Crop into cookable rice. Paddy fields remain useful for experiencing the farming mechanics, but survival food should prioritize Wild Rice drops.
