# Pot and Dirt Cookstove

<block id="ashihara:pot"/>

The Pot has `4` input slots, `1` output slot, and `10000 mB` of fluid capacity. Sneak-use opens its 3D interface. Empty-hand interaction on the top removes or replaces the lid. If a recipe requires open air or sealing, verify the lid before heating.

<block id="ashihara:dirt_cookstove"/>

The Dirt Cookstove occupies two blocks of length. Insert fuel and ignite it with flint and steel. While burning it supplies exactly `HIGH` heat; sneak-use with an empty hand extinguishes it. Every real Pot recipe currently requires `HIGH`, so another heat source should not be assumed equivalent.

## Cooking sequence

1. Leave the Cookstove's full two-block space clear and place the Pot.
2. Add the recipe's solid and fluid ingredients.
3. Set the lid, insert fuel, and ignite the stove.
4. Remove the dish from the output and extinguish the stove when finished.

::: warning
Neither the Pot nor Dirt Cookstove currently has a crafting recipe. Unless a modpack provides them, survival players cannot establish this cooking chain.
:::
