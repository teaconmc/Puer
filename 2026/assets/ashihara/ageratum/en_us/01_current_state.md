# Current State

Ashihara `0.0.0.15` targets Minecraft `26.1.2` and requires GeckoLib `4.7.3` or newer. It registers hundreds of items, more than one hundred blocks, and farming, cooking, fermentation, and miniature-building systems. It has no mod configuration and no custom commands.

## Survival boundaries

Wild Rice naturally appears in plains, and great cherry trees have an intended forest entry. Many building components, furniture pieces, Pots, Dirt Cookstoves, and fermentation vessels have no crafting recipe, however. A modpack must add sources or they remain Creative-only. Other crop seeds were intended to drop from grass, but the global loot-modifier list is empty, so those drops do not enter normal play.

The bundled Guidebook uses a 3D page system, but its current Chinese and English pages are debug or placeholder text rather than dependable instructions. These pages describe behavior found in registrations, recipes, and interaction code.

::: note
Test whether the pack supplies crop seeds and recipe-less equipment before planning a long survival base. The Creative inventory is also the most complete current catalog for the very large component set.
:::
