# Ashihara

Ashihara combines rice farming, Japanese food, fermentation vessels, miniature building components, and placeable furniture. The current build contains both substantial systems and disconnected survival sources or debug placeholders. This guide distinguishes them explicitly.

## Reading order

1. [Current state](01_current_state): dependencies, scope, and survival availability.
2. [Wild rice and seedlings](02_wild_rice_and_seedlings): find Wild Rice and understand the two rice routes.
3. [Paddy fields and water](03_paddy_fields_and_water): establish fields with the Iron Wide Hoe.
4. [Harvest and rice processing](04_harvest_and_rice_processing): drying, paddy piles, and the route that actually reaches food.
5. [Salt and other crops](05_salt_and_other_crops): produce salt and account for missing seed sources.
6. [Mortar and Cutting Board](06_mortar_and_cutting_board): grind and cut ingredients manually.
7. [Pot and Dirt Cookstove](07_pot_and_cookstove): high-heat cooking and lid handling.
8. [Fermentation](08_fermentation): use basins, vats, and large vats.
9. [Food, tableware, and chopsticks](09_food_tableware_and_chopsticks): plate food, switch eating mode, and use containers.
10. [Miniature building components](10_building_components): fine placement, removal, and material upgrades.
11. [Walls, roofs, and materials](11_walls_roofs_and_materials): select component families and manage shared space.
12. [Furniture and grid snapping](12_furniture_and_grid_snap): place small furniture efficiently.
13. [Wood, lighting, and world generation](13_wood_lighting_and_worldgen): cherry, maple, and natural content.
14. [Tools, guidebook, and known issues](14_tools_guidebook_and_issues): keys, debug material, and the issue checklist.

::: warning
The current paddy-field harvest reaches Dried Rice Crop, which has no downstream recipe. The completable food route starts with Paddy dropped by Wild Rice. Do not commit a large farm to the disconnected route until the installed version fixes it.
:::
