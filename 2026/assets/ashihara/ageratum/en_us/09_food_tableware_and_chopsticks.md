# Food, Tableware, and Chopsticks

Rice can become Onigiri, basic Sushi, and other dishes. Some foods use standard crafting recipes, while more involved dishes depend on Pot cooking, cutting, or fermentation.

## Tableware interaction

Bowls, dishes, and similar furniture components occupy a shared composite block and can hold food or fluid. Inventory-click interactions insert food into tableware or retrieve it; exact capacity depends on the piece. Snap the empty piece into position before filling it to reduce accidental interaction with a neighboring component.

Press `Alt+C` to toggle eating mode for filled tableware. With the mode off, interaction favors placement and content management; with it on, interaction favors consumption. Chopsticks work with the mod's food component, but verify the current mode at a shared table so a display dish is not eaten accidentally.

::: tip
Many tableware items have no survival recipe. A building-focused pack can distribute a basic place setting while reserving food ingredients for the actual cooking progression.
:::
