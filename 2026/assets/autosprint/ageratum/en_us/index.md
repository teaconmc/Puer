# Orga's AutoSprint

Orga's AutoSprint adds MMORPG-style auto-running to Minecraft: press one button and your character keeps walking forward on its own until you take over or cancel. It is a **client-side only** convenience mod — no items, no blocks, no commands, and nothing is sent to the server.
