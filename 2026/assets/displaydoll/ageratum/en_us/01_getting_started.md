# Getting Started

<item id="displaydoll:player_doll"/>

## Obtaining a Player Doll

The current resources contain no Player Doll recipe, so it cannot be crafted normally in Survival. The Display Doll creative tab supplies nine presets bound to the vanilla default characters `alex`, `ari`, `efe`, `kai`, `makena`, `noor`, `steve`, `sunny`, and `zuri`.

The presets differ only in their player profiles; after placement they all use the same editing and pose features. To obtain one without a preset profile, use:

`/give @s displaydoll:player_doll`

## Placement and orientation

Place a Player Doll like a normal block. It selects one of `16` horizontal rotation segments from the placing player's direction, so facing the intended display direction before placement is usually easier than replacing it later.

The doll's collision shape is smaller than a full block. It behaves like a light decorative block with wool-like sounds and breaks quickly by hand. Skin, pose, and overhead text do not change its collision.

## Creative-mode restriction

Placement itself does not require Creative mode, but both later interactions do:

- Opening the player and text editor.
- Sneak-right-clicking to cycle the pose.

For a Survival-world exhibit, have a Creative player finish the setup before other players visit it.

## Wearing the item

The Player Doll item explicitly supports the head equipment slot. It is not armor and provides no defensive attributes; this use is purely visual.
