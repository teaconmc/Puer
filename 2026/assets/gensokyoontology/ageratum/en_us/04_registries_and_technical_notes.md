# Good to Know

A short page of things that will otherwise surprise you.

## Blocks do not drop when broken

Everything in this mod is creative-mode content. **Placing a block spends it** — mining it back gives you nothing.

If you are building a Gensokyo-themed area, keep spares in your inventory rather than expecting to recover what you place.

## Nothing is craftable

There are no recipes anywhere in this mod, so the two creative tabs are your only source:

| Tab | Contents |
|:---|:---|
| **Gensokyo Ontology: Items** | Blocks, materials, decoration |
| **Gensokyo Ontology: Combat** | Danmaku, spell cards, weapons |

The Danmaku Table has its own 5×5 crafting grid for building bullet patterns — that is separate from the vanilla crafting system and is the one place in the mod where you *do* assemble something. See [Danmaku and spell cards](02_danmaku_and_spellcards).

## ⚠️ Seven items show raw text on English and Japanese clients

::: danger
**The seven Inyo Jade items are only named correctly in Simplified Chinese.**

On an English or Japanese client they display as unresolved text like `item.gensokyoontology.inyo_jade_blue` instead of a readable name. All seven colours are affected: black, red, yellow, green, aqua, blue and purple.

The items themselves work normally — this is a naming problem, not a broken item. **If you want them to read properly, play in Simplified Chinese.**
:::

## Status effect names you may see listed

Six status effects have names defined — Love Effect, Hypnosis, Mania, Depression, Hakurei Bless and Imprisoned — but **none of them are currently active in the mod**. You will not encounter them in play.

Character skills and spell cards produce their effects through other means, so the gameplay is unaffected. The names are simply left over from content that is not switched on.
