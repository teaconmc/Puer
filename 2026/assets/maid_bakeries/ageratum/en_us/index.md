# Maid Bakeries

Maid Bakeries is a bridge addon: it teaches **Touhou Little Maid** maids to operate the machines from the **Bakeries** mod. Give a maid a written order and she will work through it — blending, baking, kneading dough, or crafting — and she can also slice cakes and bread with a bread knife.

It adds no machines of its own. Everything it does is instructions for maids about blocks that belong to Bakeries.

## Read this first

**Both parent mods are required, but neither is declared in the mod metadata.** Touhou Little Maid and Bakeries must be installed. If either is missing you will not get a friendly "missing dependency" screen — the game crashes while loading classes. See [01 Getting started](01_getting_started).
