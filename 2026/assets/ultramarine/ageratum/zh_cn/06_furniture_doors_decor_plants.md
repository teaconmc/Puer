# 家具、门窗、陈设与植物

除结构方块外，群青还通过多个创造页签为室内填充家具、门窗、陈设、灯具与植物。

## 家具（`ultramarine:furniture`）

- 带物品栏的柜橱：`ultramarine:oak_cabinet`、`ultramarine:warped_cabinet`、`ultramarine:ebony_cabinet`。
- 桌子：`ultramarine:large_tea_table`、`ultramarine:chess_table`、`ultramarine:high_table_with_white_top`、`ultramarine:censer_table`、`ultramarine:porcelain_inlaid_table`、`ultramarine:small_table`、`ultramarine:small_ebony_table`、`ultramarine:large_table`、`ultramarine:long_table`。
- 椅、坐具与床（经 `seat` 实体坐入）：`ultramarine:ebony_chair`、`ultramarine:chair_with_yellow_cushion`、`ultramarine:painted_chair`、`ultramarine:wooden_stool`、`ultramarine:porcelain_inlaid_grand_chair`、`ultramarine:yellow_cushion`、`ultramarine:oak_bed`、`ultramarine:ebony_bed`。
- 屏风：`ultramarine:large_landscape_painting_screen`、`ultramarine:painted_screen`。

## 门窗与窗帘（`ultramarine:windows_and_doors`）

- 门：`ultramarine:carved_wooden_door`、`ultramarine:song_wooden_door`。
- 窗：`ultramarine:song_wooden_window`、`ultramarine:royal_rosewood_window`、`ultramarine:tall_royal_rosewood_window`、`ultramarine:glazed_tile_grid_window`、`ultramarine:stone_flower_window`。
- 门扇与窗饰：`ultramarine:knocker`、`ultramarine:rosewood_panel`、`ultramarine:rosewood_door_panel`、`ultramarine:large_rosewood_panel`、`ultramarine:carved_wooden_door_panel`、`ultramarine:plaque`、`ultramarine:paper_strip_seal`。
- 窗帘：`ultramarine:large_blue_curtain`、`ultramarine:medium_blue_curtain`、`ultramarine:small_blue_curtain`、`ultramarine:red_curtain`、`ultramarine:red_curtain_corner`、`ultramarine:bamboo_curtain`。

## 室内与庭院陈设（`ultramarine:decorations`）

- 书房：`ultramarine:abacus`、`ultramarine:brush_tools`、`ultramarine:brush_and_inkstone`、`ultramarine:ruler`、`ultramarine:painting_scroll`、`ultramarine:book_stack`、`ultramarine:bamboo_slips`。
- 房间陈设：`ultramarine:back_cushion`、`ultramarine:chessboard`、`ultramarine:blue_and_white_porcelain_vase`、`ultramarine:large_blue_and_white_porcelain_vase`、`ultramarine:blue_and_white_porcelain_bowl`、`ultramarine:bronze_mirror`、`ultramarine:memorial_tablet`、`ultramarine:bronze_censer`、`ultramarine:royal_censer`、`ultramarine:porcelain_teapot`、`ultramarine:jade_pendant`、`ultramarine:imperial_jade_seal`、`ultramarine:long_pillow`、`ultramarine:wind_chime`。
- 花盆：`ultramarine:blue_and_white_porcelain_flowerpot`、`ultramarine:terracotta_flowerpot`、`ultramarine:black_iron_flowerpot`、`ultramarine:blue_porcelain_flowerpot`。
- 庭院：`ultramarine:sack`、`ultramarine:bamboo_tea_basket`、`ultramarine:straw_hat`、`ultramarine:knife`、`ultramarine:sundial`、`ultramarine:broom`、`ultramarine:stone_pedestal`、`ultramarine:terracotta_pot`、`ultramarine:chinese_herbs_bag`、`ultramarine:fruit_box`、`ultramarine:wooden_crate`、`ultramarine:gunny_sack`、`ultramarine:bronze_ding`、`ultramarine:carriage`、`ultramarine:wooden_poles`、`ultramarine:teahouse_flag`、`ultramarine:knife_rest`。
- 墙面挂饰：`ultramarine:long_hanging_painting`、`ultramarine:white_landscape_painting`、`ultramarine:horizontal_calligraphy`、`ultramarine:horizontal_landscape_painting`、`ultramarine:portrait`、`ultramarine:hanging_painting_fan`、`ultramarine:sincere_calligraphy`。
- 食物与节庆：`ultramarine:food_hamper`、`ultramarine:plated_mooncakes`、`ultramarine:plated_mung_bean_cakes`、`ultramarine:plated_ham`、`ultramarine:plated_fish`、`ultramarine:wine_pot`、`ultramarine:xiaolongbao`、`ultramarine:couplet`、`ultramarine:couplet_top`、`ultramarine:fu_mark`，以及 `ultramarine:icicle` 与 `ultramarine:large_icicle`。

## 灯具（`ultramarine:lamps`）

- 灯笼：`ultramarine:octagonal_palace_lantern`、`ultramarine:square_palace_lantern`、`ultramarine:small_red_lantern`、`ultramarine:white_sky_lantern`、`ultramarine:red_sky_lantern`、`ultramarine:yellow_sky_lantern`。
- 灯与路灯：`ultramarine:standing_lamp`、`ultramarine:small_standing_lamp`、`ultramarine:stone_lamp`、`ultramarine:red_lantern_streetlight`、`ultramarine:hanging_red_lantern_streetlight`、`ultramarine:streetlight_pole`。
- 烛台：`ultramarine:red_candle`、`ultramarine:tricolor_candlestick`、`ultramarine:jade_candlestick`。

## 植物（`ultramarine:plants`）

- 荷花：`ultramarine:small_lotus_leaf`、`ultramarine:large_lotus_leaf`、`ultramarine:lotus_bud`、`ultramarine:medium_lotus` 及叶簇。
- 常春藤与落叶：`ultramarine:small_red_ivy`、`ultramarine:medium_red_ivy`、`ultramarine:large_red_ivy`（另有黄、绿常春藤），以及 `ultramarine:small_leaf_pile`、`ultramarine:medium_leaf_pile`、`ultramarine:large_leaf_pile`。
- 盆景：`ultramarine:small_greeting_pine_bonsai`、`ultramarine:large_greeting_pine_bonsai`、`ultramarine:red_plum_bonsai`、`ultramarine:bamboo_bonsai`、`ultramarine:red_coral_bonsai`、瓷瓶盆景（`ultramarine:small_white_porcelain_vase_bonsai`、`ultramarine:tall_blue_and_white_porcelain_bonsai` 等），以及 `ultramarine:medium_bonsai`、`ultramarine:large_bonsai` 与 `ultramarine:lotus_leaves_bowl`。
