# 大木构架

`ultramarine:decorative_blocks` 创造页签收纳传统中式木构架构件与彩绘装饰，赋予建筑结构与纹饰。

## 斗拱

斗拱覆盖每种原版木材及花梨木：`ultramarine:oak_bracket`、`ultramarine:spruce_bracket`、`ultramarine:birch_bracket`、`ultramarine:jungle_bracket`、`ultramarine:acacia_bracket`、`ultramarine:dark_oak_bracket`、`ultramarine:crimson_bracket`、`ultramarine:warped_bracket`、`ultramarine:mangrove_bracket`、`ultramarine:cherry_bracket` 与 `ultramarine:varnished_rosewood_bracket`。鎏金形式为 `ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_bracket`。

## 彩绘木

四色简式彩绘木：`ultramarine:red_carved_wood`、`ultramarine:blue_carved_wood`、`ultramarine:cyan_carved_wood` 与 `ultramarine:green_carved_wood`，每种配台阶与楼梯变体（如 `ultramarine:red_carved_wooden_slab`、`ultramarine:red_carved_wooden_stairs`）。

复色变体包括 `ultramarine:orange_and_blue_carved_wood`、`ultramarine:green_and_blue_carved_wood`、`ultramarine:dark_green_and_dark_blue_carved_wood`、`ultramarine:black_white_green_carved_wood`、`ultramarine:red_and_green_carved_wood`、`ultramarine:blue_white_red_carved_wood`、`ultramarine:blue_and_yellow_carved_wood_variant_1` / `_variant_2` / `_variant_3` 与 `ultramarine:yellow_green_blue_carved_wood`。彩绘地毯为 `ultramarine:red_carved_carpet` 与 `ultramarine:blue_carved_carpet`。

## 深色橡与鎏金木

- `ultramarine:carved_dark_oak_beam`、`ultramarine:carved_dark_oak_beam_edge`。
- 鎏金：`ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak`、`ultramarine:chiseled_gilded_dark_oak`、`ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_slab`、`ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_stairs`。

## 柱

柱子包括 `ultramarine:carved_red_pillar` 及其柱础/柱头 `ultramarine:carved_red_pillar_base`、`ultramarine:carved_red_pillar_head`；`ultramarine:spruce_pillar_base`、`ultramarine:stripped_dark_oak_pillar_base`、`ultramarine:stone_pillar_base`；青白套件 `ultramarine:cyan_and_white_pillar_base`、`ultramarine:cyan_and_white_decorated_pillar`、`ultramarine:cyan_and_white_pillar_head`；以及五个鎏金柱头 `ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_pillar_head_variant_1` 至 `_variant_5`。

## 方心、爪头与栱头

彩绘梁枋与斗拱饰件：

- 方心 —— 大量配色组合，例如 `ultramarine:green_fangxin`、`ultramarine:blue_fangxin`、`ultramarine:blue_and_white_fangxin`、`ultramarine:dark_blue_fangxin`、`ultramarine:cyan_and_blue_fangxin`，以及 `_edge` 形式如 `ultramarine:blue_fangxin_edge`。历代样式包括 `ultramarine:ming_blue_gilded_jinzhuomo_shinianyu_xuanzi_fangxin`、`ultramarine:yuan_nianyuzhuang_fangxin` 与 `ultramarine:yuan_patterned_wuchaizhuang_fangxin`。
- 爪头 —— `ultramarine:blue_zhaotou_edge`、`ultramarine:green_and_blue_zhaotou`、`ultramarine:blue_and_green_double_layered_zhaotou`、`ultramarine:cyan_and_red_zhaotou`、`ultramarine:red_and_blue_zhaotou`、`ultramarine:yuan_nianyuzhuang_zhaotou` 等。
- 栱头 —— `ultramarine:green_blue_black_gutou`、`ultramarine:blue_green_yellow_gutou`、`ultramarine:blue_and_yellow_gutou`、`ultramarine:yuan_nianyuzhuang_gutou`。

## 椽与梁

每种木材都有素椽与椽头，例如 `ultramarine:oak_rafter`、`ultramarine:oak_rafter_end`、`ultramarine:spruce_rafter`、`ultramarine:cherry_rafter` 与 `ultramarine:cherry_rafter_end`。装饰椽包括 `ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_rafter`、`ultramarine:blue_tiger_eye_rafter` 与 `ultramarine:green_wanzi_rafter`。梁头包括 `ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_beam_head` 与 `ultramarine:qing_green_beam_head`。

## 额枋与雀替

- 额枋：`ultramarine:red_and_cyan_carved_architrave`、`ultramarine:red_and_blue_carved_architrave`（均含台阶）、`ultramarine:red_carved_architrave`，以及团花台阶 `ultramarine:empty_tuanhua_slab`、`ultramarine:full_tuanhua_slab`、`ultramarine:red_and_cyan_tuanhua_slab`；另有 `ultramarine:white_and_red_slab`。
- 雀替与挂落：`ultramarine:long_gilded_dark_oak_queti`、`ultramarine:horizontal_gilded_dark_oak_queti`、`ultramarine:central_gilded_dark_oak_queti`、`ultramarine:vertical_gilded_dark_oak_queti`、`ultramarine:wooden_queti`、`ultramarine:wooden_gualuo`、`ultramarine:large_wooden_gualuo`，以及石刻件 `ultramarine:carved_stone_queti`、`ultramarine:carved_stone_panel`、`ultramarine:carved_stone_beam`。

## 装饰纹样与屋面饰件

- 墙面与彩绘纹样：`ultramarine:yellow_carved_pattern`、`ultramarine:circular_yellow_carved_pattern`、`ultramarine:golden_dragon_fangxin_pattern`、`ultramarine:blue_fangxin_pattern`、`ultramarine:light_blue_su_style_caihua`、`ultramarine:loong_tuanhua`、`ultramarine:glazed_central_wall_pattern` 与 `ultramarine:grass_lucky_motif_1` / `_2` / `_3`。
- 垂花：`ultramarine:blue_and_green_chuihua`、`ultramarine:cyan_and_yellow_chuihua`、`ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_chuihua`。
- 屋面饰件：`ultramarine:golden_glazed_roof_charm`、`ultramarine:green_glazed_roof_charm`、`ultramarine:dark_prismarine_chiwei`，以及藻井 `ultramarine:qing_golden_dragon_ceiling`。

## 栏杆与守门石狮

- 栏杆：`ultramarine:white_marble_railing`、`ultramarine:white_marble_railing_slant`、`ultramarine:carved_wooden_railing`、`ultramarine:red_wooden_railing`、`ultramarine:red_wooden_railing_edge`、`ultramarine:wooden_railing`、`ultramarine:wooden_railing_variant`。
- 守门狮：`ultramarine:small_wooden_guardian_lion`、`ultramarine:small_stone_guardian_lion`、`ultramarine:small_jade_guardian_lion`、`ultramarine:small_yellow_glazed_guardian_lion`、`ultramarine:small_green_glazed_guardian_lion`，以及 `ultramarine:xumi_stone_monolith`。

## 蔬菜篮装饰

此页签也收纳装饰性农产品篮：`ultramarine:cabbage_basket`、`ultramarine:celery_basket`、`ultramarine:orange_basket`、`ultramarine:apple_basket`、`ultramarine:eggplant_basket`、`ultramarine:pear_basket`，以及 `ultramarine:bookshelf` 与 `ultramarine:bookshelf_variant`。
