# Furniture, Doors, Decor, and Plants

Beyond the structural blocks, Ultramarine fills interiors with furniture, doors and windows, room decor, lanterns, and plants across several creative tabs.

## Furniture (`ultramarine:furniture`)

- Cabinets with inventory: `ultramarine:oak_cabinet`, `ultramarine:warped_cabinet`, `ultramarine:ebony_cabinet`.
- Tables: `ultramarine:large_tea_table`, `ultramarine:chess_table`, `ultramarine:high_table_with_white_top`, `ultramarine:censer_table`, `ultramarine:porcelain_inlaid_table`, `ultramarine:small_table`, `ultramarine:small_ebony_table`, `ultramarine:large_table`, `ultramarine:long_table`.
- Chairs, seats, and beds (seating via the `seat` entity): `ultramarine:ebony_chair`, `ultramarine:chair_with_yellow_cushion`, `ultramarine:painted_chair`, `ultramarine:wooden_stool`, `ultramarine:porcelain_inlaid_grand_chair`, `ultramarine:yellow_cushion`, `ultramarine:oak_bed`, `ultramarine:ebony_bed`.
- Screens: `ultramarine:large_landscape_painting_screen`, `ultramarine:painted_screen`.
