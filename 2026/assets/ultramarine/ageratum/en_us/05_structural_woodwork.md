# Structural Woodwork

The `ultramarine:decorative_blocks` creative tab holds the traditional Chinese timber-frame members and painted decorations that give buildings their structure and ornament.

## Brackets

Timber brackets cover every vanilla wood plus rosewood: `ultramarine:oak_bracket`, `ultramarine:spruce_bracket`, `ultramarine:birch_bracket`, `ultramarine:jungle_bracket`, `ultramarine:acacia_bracket`, `ultramarine:dark_oak_bracket`, `ultramarine:crimson_bracket`, `ultramarine:warped_bracket`, `ultramarine:mangrove_bracket`, `ultramarine:cherry_bracket`, and `ultramarine:varnished_rosewood_bracket`. The gilded form is `ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_bracket`.

## Carved Wood

Simple carved wood in four colors: `ultramarine:red_carved_wood`, `ultramarine:blue_carved_wood`, `ultramarine:cyan_carved_wood`, and `ultramarine:green_carved_wood`, each with slab and stairs variants (for example `ultramarine:red_carved_wooden_slab`, `ultramarine:red_carved_wooden_stairs`).

Mixed painted variants include `ultramarine:orange_and_blue_carved_wood`, `ultramarine:green_and_blue_carved_wood`, `ultramarine:dark_green_and_dark_blue_carved_wood`, `ultramarine:black_white_green_carved_wood`, `ultramarine:red_and_green_carved_wood`, `ultramarine:blue_white_red_carved_wood`, `ultramarine:blue_and_yellow_carved_wood_variant_1` / `_variant_2` / `_variant_3`, and `ultramarine:yellow_green_blue_carved_wood`. Carved carpets are `ultramarine:red_carved_carpet` and `ultramarine:blue_carved_carpet`.

## Dark Oak and Gilded Wood

- `ultramarine:carved_dark_oak_beam`, `ultramarine:carved_dark_oak_beam_edge`.
- Gilded: `ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak`, `ultramarine:chiseled_gilded_dark_oak`, `ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_slab`, `ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_stairs`.

## Pillars

Pillars include `ultramarine:carved_red_pillar` with `ultramarine:carved_red_pillar_base` and `ultramarine:carved_red_pillar_head`; `ultramarine:spruce_pillar_base`, `ultramarine:stripped_dark_oak_pillar_base`, `ultramarine:stone_pillar_base`; the cyan-and-white set `ultramarine:cyan_and_white_pillar_base`, `ultramarine:cyan_and_white_decorated_pillar`, `ultramarine:cyan_and_white_pillar_head`; and five gilded heads `ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_pillar_head_variant_1` through `_variant_5`.

## Fangxin, Zhaotou, and Gutou

Painted ceiling-beam and bracket ornaments:

- Fangxin (方心) — many color combinations, for example `ultramarine:green_fangxin`, `ultramarine:blue_fangxin`, `ultramarine:blue_and_white_fangxin`, `ultramarine:dark_blue_fangxin`, `ultramarine:cyan_and_blue_fangxin`, plus `_edge` forms such as `ultramarine:blue_fangxin_edge`. Historical-era pieces include `ultramarine:ming_blue_gilded_jinzhuomo_shinianyu_xuanzi_fangxin`, `ultramarine:yuan_nianyuzhuang_fangxin`, and `ultramarine:yuan_patterned_wuchaizhuang_fangxin`.
- Zhaotou (爪头) — `ultramarine:blue_zhaotou_edge`, `ultramarine:green_and_blue_zhaotou`, `ultramarine:blue_and_green_double_layered_zhaotou`, `ultramarine:cyan_and_red_zhaotou`, `ultramarine:red_and_blue_zhaotou`, `ultramarine:yuan_nianyuzhuang_zhaotou`, and more.
- Gutou (栱头) — `ultramarine:green_blue_black_gutou`, `ultramarine:blue_green_yellow_gutou`, `ultramarine:blue_and_yellow_gutou`, `ultramarine:yuan_nianyuzhuang_gutou`.

## Rafters and Beams

Plain rafters exist for every wood, each with an end piece, for example `ultramarine:oak_rafter`, `ultramarine:oak_rafter_end`, `ultramarine:spruce_rafter`, `ultramarine:cherry_rafter`, and `ultramarine:cherry_rafter_end`. Decorated rafters include `ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_rafter`, `ultramarine:blue_tiger_eye_rafter`, and `ultramarine:green_wanzi_rafter`. Beam heads include `ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_beam_head` and `ultramarine:qing_green_beam_head`.

## Architrave and Queti

- Architrave: `ultramarine:red_and_cyan_carved_architrave`, `ultramarine:red_and_blue_carved_architrave` (+ slabs), `ultramarine:red_carved_architrave`, and tuanhua slabs `ultramarine:empty_tuanhua_slab`, `ultramarine:full_tuanhua_slab`, `ultramarine:red_and_cyan_tuanhua_slab`; plus `ultramarine:white_and_red_slab`.
- Queti (雀替) and gualuo: `ultramarine:long_gilded_dark_oak_queti`, `ultramarine:horizontal_gilded_dark_oak_queti`, `ultramarine:central_gilded_dark_oak_queti`, `ultramarine:vertical_gilded_dark_oak_queti`, `ultramarine:wooden_queti`, `ultramarine:wooden_gualuo`, `ultramarine:large_wooden_gualuo`, and carved-stone pieces `ultramarine:carved_stone_queti`, `ultramarine:carved_stone_panel`, `ultramarine:carved_stone_beam`.

## Decorative Patterns and Roof Trim

- Wall and painted patterns: `ultramarine:yellow_carved_pattern`, `ultramarine:circular_yellow_carved_pattern`, `ultramarine:golden_dragon_fangxin_pattern`, `ultramarine:blue_fangxin_pattern`, `ultramarine:light_blue_su_style_caihua`, `ultramarine:loong_tuanhua`, `ultramarine:glazed_central_wall_pattern`, and `ultramarine:grass_lucky_motif_1` / `_2` / `_3`.
- Chuihua (垂花): `ultramarine:blue_and_green_chuihua`, `ultramarine:cyan_and_yellow_chuihua`, `ultramarine:gilded_dark_oak_chuihua`.
- Roof trim: `ultramarine:golden_glazed_roof_charm`, `ultramarine:green_glazed_roof_charm`, `ultramarine:dark_prismarine_chiwei`, and the ceiling `ultramarine:qing_golden_dragon_ceiling`.

## Railings and Guardian Lions

- Railings: `ultramarine:white_marble_railing`, `ultramarine:white_marble_railing_slant`, `ultramarine:carved_wooden_railing`, `ultramarine:red_wooden_railing`, `ultramarine:red_wooden_railing_edge`, `ultramarine:wooden_railing`, `ultramarine:wooden_railing_variant`.
- Guardian lions: `ultramarine:small_wooden_guardian_lion`, `ultramarine:small_stone_guardian_lion`, `ultramarine:small_jade_guardian_lion`, `ultramarine:small_yellow_glazed_guardian_lion`, `ultramarine:small_green_glazed_guardian_lion`, plus the `ultramarine:xumi_stone_monolith`.

## Basket Decor

Decorative produce baskets sit in this tab too: `ultramarine:cabbage_basket`, `ultramarine:celery_basket`, `ultramarine:orange_basket`, `ultramarine:apple_basket`, `ultramarine:eggplant_basket`, `ultramarine:pear_basket`, plus `ultramarine:bookshelf` and `ultramarine:bookshelf_variant`.
