# Roofing and Ridges

Ultramarine provides six colored roof-tile families and a matching roof ridge system, all in the `ultramarine:building_blocks` creative tab.

## Roof Tiles

Each color has a normal tile, a stairs tile, and an edge tile:

| Color | Tiles |
|:---|:---|
| Gray | `ultramarine:gray_roof_tiles`, `ultramarine:gray_roof_tile_stairs`, `ultramarine:gray_roof_tile_edge` |
| Yellow | `ultramarine:yellow_roof_tiles`, `ultramarine:yellow_roof_tile_stairs`, `ultramarine:yellow_roof_tile_edge` |
| Green | `ultramarine:green_roof_tiles`, `ultramarine:green_roof_tile_stairs`, `ultramarine:green_roof_tile_edge` |
| Blue | `ultramarine:blue_roof_tiles`, `ultramarine:blue_roof_tile_stairs`, `ultramarine:blue_roof_tile_edge` |
| Cyan | `ultramarine:cyan_roof_tiles`, `ultramarine:cyan_roof_tile_stairs`, `ultramarine:cyan_roof_tile_edge` |
| Black | `ultramarine:black_roof_tiles`, `ultramarine:black_roof_tile_stairs`, `ultramarine:black_roof_tile_edge` |

## Roof Ridges

Roof ridges cap the ridge line and come in two color sets (black and yellow). Black ridge pieces include:

- `ultramarine:black_roof_ridge_upper` and `ultramarine:black_roof_ridge_lower` — the two ridge profiles.
- `ultramarine:black_roof_ridge_connection` and `ultramarine:black_main_roof_ridge_connection` — junction pieces.
- `ultramarine:black_roof_ridge_upper_slab`, `ultramarine:black_main_roof_ridge_slab`, `ultramarine:black_main_roof_ridge_panel`, `ultramarine:black_main_roof_ridge_plate`, and `ultramarine:black_main_roof_ridge_stairs` — slab/panel/plate/stairs forms.

The yellow set mirrors the black one with `ultramarine:yellow_roof_ridge_upper`, `ultramarine:yellow_roof_ridge_lower`, `ultramarine:yellow_roof_ridge_connection`, `ultramarine:yellow_main_roof_ridge_connection`, `ultramarine:yellow_roof_ridge_upper_slab`, `ultramarine:yellow_main_roof_ridge_slab`, `ultramarine:yellow_main_roof_ridge_panel`, `ultramarine:yellow_main_roof_ridge_plate`, and `ultramarine:yellow_main_roof_ridge_stairs`.
