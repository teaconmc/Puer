# Building Blocks

The `ultramarine:building_blocks` creative tab groups the masonry, stone, flooring, and wooden blocks that form walls, floors, and foundations.

## Bricks

- `ultramarine:cyan_bricks`, `ultramarine:black_bricks`, `ultramarine:brownish_red_stone_bricks`, `ultramarine:white_and_pink_mixed_bricks`, `ultramarine:green_weathered_bricks`.

Each brick family also has slab, stairs, and wall variants, for example `ultramarine:cyan_brick_slab`, `ultramarine:cyan_brick_stairs`, and `ultramarine:cyan_brick_wall`.

## Stones

- `ultramarine:pale_yellow_stone`, `ultramarine:variegated_rocks`, `ultramarine:weathered_stone`, `ultramarine:polished_weathered_stone`, `ultramarine:green_weathered_stone`.

Slab and stairs forms are available for the main stone sets (`ultramarine:weathered_stone_slab`, `ultramarine:weathered_stone_stairs`, `ultramarine:polished_weathered_stone_wall`, and so on).

## Floor Tiles

The tile families come in full blocks plus slab/stairs/wall forms:

- Cyan tiles — `ultramarine:light_cyan_floor_tile`, `ultramarine:cyan_floor_tile`, `ultramarine:dark_cyan_floor_tile`, plus directional `ultramarine:layered_cyan_floor_tiles`, `ultramarine:vertical_cyan_floor_tiles`, `ultramarine:mixed_cyan_floor_tiles`, and variants `ultramarine:chiseled_cyan_floor_tile` and `ultramarine:cut_cyan_floor_tiles`.
- Red tiles — `ultramarine:red_floor_tile`, `ultramarine:weathered_red_stone_tile`, and `ultramarine:blue_and_black_tile`.
- Fired and weathered — `ultramarine:green_glazed_tiles`, `ultramarine:black_floor_tiles` with `ultramarine:black_floor_tiles_little_mossy`, `ultramarine:black_floor_tiles_moderate_mossy`, and `ultramarine:black_floor_tiles_very_mossy` moss-growth stages.

## Wooden and Bamboo

- `ultramarine:rosewood_planks` with `ultramarine:rosewood_slab`, `ultramarine:rosewood_stairs`, and `ultramarine:rosewood_fence`; `ultramarine:varnished_rosewood` is the lacquered form.
- `ultramarine:bamboo_mat` with `ultramarine:bamboo_mat_slab` and `ultramarine:bamboo_mat_stairs`.
