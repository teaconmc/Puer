# Materials and Mining

The `ultramarine:materials` creative tab collects the raw ores, refined products, and intermediate materials used across the mod.

## Ores and Refined Materials

Four ores with their refined forms:

| Ore block | Refined products |
|:---|:---|
| `ultramarine:jade_ore`, `ultramarine:deepslate_jade_ore` | `ultramarine:jade`, plus `ultramarine:jade_block` and `ultramarine:jade_parts` |
| `ultramarine:magnesite_ore`, `ultramarine:deepslate_magnesite_ore` | `ultramarine:magnesite`, `ultramarine:magnesite_dust` |
| `ultramarine:hematite_ore`, `ultramarine:deepslate_hematite_ore` | `ultramarine:raw_hematite`, `ultramarine:hematite_dust` |
| `ultramarine:nether_cobalt_ore` | `ultramarine:raw_cobalt`, `ultramarine:cobalt_dust` |

`ultramarine:bronze_ingot` and `ultramarine:bronze_dust` extend the bronze chain, with `ultramarine:bronze_block` for storage.

## Bricks and Roof Tiles

Brick families are prepared as raw clay (`ultramarine:unfired_clay_brick`), dyed unfired variants (`ultramarine:unfired_cyan_brick`, `ultramarine:unfired_black_brick`, `ultramarine:unfired_brownish_red_stone_brick`), and fired results (`ultramarine:fired_brick`). Finished bricks are `ultramarine:cyan_brick`, `ultramarine:black_brick`, and `ultramarine:brownish_red_stone_brick`. `ultramarine:prismarine_dust` is a secondary material here.

Roof tiles follow a similar unfired-to-fired path: `ultramarine:unfired_roof_tile` becomes the colored tiles `ultramarine:gray_roof_tile`, `ultramarine:yellow_roof_tile`, `ultramarine:green_roof_tile`, `ultramarine:blue_roof_tile`, `ultramarine:cyan_roof_tile`, and `ultramarine:black_roof_tile`.

## Planks and Frames

Polished planks exist for every vanilla wood plus the mod's own woods: `ultramarine:polished_oak_plank`, `ultramarine:polished_birch_plank`, `ultramarine:polished_spruce_plank`, `ultramarine:polished_jungle_plank`, `ultramarine:polished_acacia_plank`, `ultramarine:polished_dark_oak_plank`, `ultramarine:polished_warped_plank`, `ultramarine:polished_crimson_plank`, `ultramarine:polished_mangrove_plank`, `ultramarine:polished_cherry_plank`, `ultramarine:polished_rosewood_plank`, and `ultramarine:polished_ebony_plank`. `ultramarine:wooden_frame` is a common building frame ingredient.

## Porcelain Materials

The porcelain chain starts with `ultramarine:calcite_dust` and `ultramarine:blue_and_white_glaze_powder`. Clay intermediates include `ultramarine:small_clay_vase`, `ultramarine:medium_clay_vase`, `ultramarine:large_clay_vase`, `ultramarine:tall_clay_vase`, `ultramarine:clay_pot`, `ultramarine:tall_clay_pot`, `ultramarine:clay_plate`, and `ultramarine:clay_bowl`. Finished porcelain ware includes white and green vases, `ultramarine:tall_blue_porcelain_vase`, `ultramarine:tall_blue_and_white_porcelain_vase`, `ultramarine:black_porcelain_plate`, and `ultramarine:blue_and_white_porcelain_plate`. Scrap returns as `ultramarine:blue_and_white_porcelain_piece`, `ultramarine:blue_and_white_porcelain_shards`, and `ultramarine:porcelain_piece`.

## Dye Powders and Templates

Sixteen dye powders (`ultramarine:white_dye_powder` through `ultramarine:black_dye_powder`) plus `ultramarine:gold_dye_powder` color the painted woodwork. Chisel templates target each decorative family: `ultramarine:carved_wood_template`, `ultramarine:carved_wool_template`, `ultramarine:fangxin_template`, `ultramarine:fangxin_edge_template`, `ultramarine:zhaotou_template`, `ultramarine:gutou_template`, `ultramarine:rafter_template`, `ultramarine:rafter_end_template`, `ultramarine:architrave_template`, and `ultramarine:caihua_template`.

## Parts and Other Materials

Generic parts combine into decor: `ultramarine:wooden_parts`, `ultramarine:gold_parts`, `ultramarine:jade_parts`, `ultramarine:porcelain_parts`, and `ultramarine:bronze_parts`. Everyday materials include `ultramarine:incense`, `ultramarine:fur`, `ultramarine:grease`, `ultramarine:silk`, `ultramarine:xuan_paper`, and `ultramarine:copper_cash_coin`.

Edible materials are also in this tab: `ultramarine:mooncake`, `ultramarine:mung_bean_cake`, `ultramarine:raw_meat`, `ultramarine:cooked_meat`, and `ultramarine:baozi`.
