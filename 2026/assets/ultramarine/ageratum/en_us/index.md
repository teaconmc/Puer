# Ultramarine

Ultramarine (群青) is a Chinese-style Minecraft mod that adds traditional architecture pieces, structural woodwork, furniture, and room decor across nine creative tabs. It is built for the NeoForge loader and is currently at version `0.8-snapshot` for Minecraft 26.1.2 (NeoForge 26.1.2.36-beta).

## Reading Route

1. [Materials and Mining](01_materials_and_mining): mine jade, magnesite, hematite, and cobalt; refine them and prepare bricks, roof tiles, porcelain, dye powders, and woodworking templates.
2. [Crafting Stations and Tools](02_crafting_stations_and_tools): the wooden mallet, the porcelain tool set, and the woodworking workbench, brick kiln, and chisel table plus their custom recipe types.
3. [Building Blocks](03_building_blocks): bricks, stones, floor tiles, and wooden planks such as rosewood and bamboo mat.
4. [Roofing and Ridges](04_roofing_and_ridges): six roof-tile colors with stairs and edge variants, plus roof ridge pieces.
5. [Structural Woodwork](05_structural_woodwork): brackets, carved wood, pillars, fangxin, zhaotou, gutou, rafters, beams, queti, chuihua, railings, and guardian lions.
6. [Furniture, Doors, Decor, and Plants](06_furniture_doors_decor_plants): cabinets and seating, doors and windows, room and courtyard decor, lamps, and plants.

## Creative Tabs

Ultramarine registers nine creative tabs, each gathering a coherent set of items:

| Tab | Representative items |
|:---|:---|
| `ultramarine:materials` | Ores, refined materials, bricks, roof tiles, porcelain, dye powders, templates |
| `ultramarine:tools` | Wooden mallet, blue-and-white porcelain tools, workstations |
| `ultramarine:building_blocks` | Bricks, stones, floor tiles, planks, roof tiles and ridges |
| `ultramarine:decorative_blocks` | Brackets, carved wood, pillars, structural trim, railings |
| `ultramarine:decorations` | Study items, porcelain, courtyard props, wall hangings, food |
| `ultramarine:furniture` | Cabinets, tables, chairs, beds, screens |
| `ultramarine:windows_and_doors` | Doors, windows, panels, curtains |
| `ultramarine:lamps` | Lanterns, lamps, streetlights, candlesticks |
| `ultramarine:plants` | Lotus, ivy, leaf piles, bonsai |
