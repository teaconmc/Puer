# Sugar powders and ingredients

## Nether and ender sugar

Vanilla sugar is the overworld sugar powder. `ccw:nether_sugar` and `ccw:ender_sugar` are the two higher-tier sugar powders used in the Candy Workshop. They also mark the machine recipes that require a specific sugar type: overworld formulas use vanilla sugar, nether formulas use `ccw:nether_sugar`, and end formulas use `ccw:ender_sugar`.

## Other ingredients

`ccw:phantom_pearl` is the main ingredient for invisibility candy, `ccw:weakness_powder` identifies weakness candy, `ccw:greedy_offering` and `ccw:defiled_offering` are end-tier luck/unluck ingredients, and `ccw:ominous_flag` identifies bad-omen candy.

## Carametal

`ccw:carametal` is the only `ccw:refining` machine recipe stored as a regular JSON recipe. It refines 8 carton milks, 8 vanilla sugar and a copper block into one carametal. Unlike gummy formulas, this recipe has no flavor slot.
