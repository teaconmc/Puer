# Service Blocks and Tools

## Trays

Plastic and Redwood trays store one cooked dish. Right-click with a cooked dish to place it; right-click an empty hand on a filled tray to take it; sneak and right-click to inspect its bound restaurant and serve-ready state. During an active business, placing a tray can draw the dish from restaurant prepared stock, and customers within 2 blocks can be served directly from a targeted tray.

## Ticket printer and blackboard

The Ticket Printer links Blackboards within 15 blocks horizontally and 4 vertically. The owner or a Game Master right-clicks the printer to link, and active customer orders are synced to linked blackboards as `order number - dish` lines. A nearby printer is required before order-board sync begins.

## Menu sign and QR order stand

Both are read-only recipe viewers. Right-clicking opens a screen listing the nearby restaurant's valid recipes with their acquisition source. If no Cash Register is detected, they report that no restaurant is nearby.

## Bell, fridge and lockers

The Bell is placed by the Entrance/Exit door marker when space allows, or manually, and plays a bell sound. The Fridge is the 27-slot stock container and also accepts placed cooked dishes on top. Storage Lockers are personal 27-slot containers that bind on placement and lock during business.

## Garbage and mop

Customers can leave Garbage blocks near their exit. A Mop cleans a Garbage block and drives away a customer (owner-restricted). Garbage that is not cleaned reduces popularity.

## Remote control

`delicacyworld:remote_control` toggles the active state of air conditioners and ceiling fans. These blocks also emit looping sounds (`fans`, `air_conditioner`) and can emit light when active.

## Decorative add-ons

The `restaurant_addon_decorative` creative tab contains 35 registered addon decoration blocks such as `addon_blue_bicycle`, `addon_yellow_bicycle`, `addon_camera`, `addon_monitor` and `addon_taikongdianti`. They are decorative scene models with no gameplay function in the current checkout.
