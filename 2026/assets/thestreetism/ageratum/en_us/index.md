# TheStreetism

TheStreetism is a street-culture decoration mod. It gives you two independent toolsets:

- **Graffiti** — spray patterns onto any solid block face with a spray can, and scrape them off again.
- **Street banners and flags** — hang a text banner across a wall, or stitch a cloth rectangle onto flag poles and edit it in a dedicated editor with fonts, colours and alignment.

Two things to know before you start: the flag half of the mod has **no crafting recipes**, and the 50 built-in graffiti patterns **ship without textures**. Both are covered below.
