# Ether Craft（以太工艺）

Ether Craft (mod id `ether_craft`, version `0.2.1`) is a mod **based on item transportation and slot placement** — "Ether Craft is a mod based on item transportation and slots placement." Developed by xypp & Yuia (MIT) under `studio.fantasyit.ether_craft`, for Minecraft 26.1.2 / NeoForge (`26.1.2.61-beta`), with JEI and GuideMe integration.

## What it offers

- **Ether resources** — a new material chain from `ether_ore` / `deepslate_ether_ore` / `nether_ether_ore` and `ether_dust`, `ether_ingot`, `ether_crystal`, `ether_block`, plus `inactivated_ether` and cheese.
- **Item-transport devices** — `ether_stream_emitter` (stream emitter), `ether_process_factory` (processing factory, 4 levels), `ether_adapt_node` (adapt node, 3 levels) move item-resource streams between slots.
- **Chips & recipes** — `process_chip` and `direct_input` chips, plus 5×5 / 7×7 / 9×9 recipe-answer items; custom `NodeProcessRecipe` / `EtherProcessFactoryRecipe` types.
- **Tools & blocks** — the `wrench`, decorative `ether_glass`, `ether_block`, food `cheese`, and world-generated ether ores.
