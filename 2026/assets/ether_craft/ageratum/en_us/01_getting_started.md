# Getting started

All Ether Craft content is grouped in the **`ether_craft_main`** creative tab (icon: `ether`).

## Ether resources

- **Mining** — `ether_ore`, `deepslate_ether_ore` and `nether_ether_ore` generate in the world (`WorldGenData`); their loot is set by the mod's loot-table datagen.
- **Refining** — mine for `ether_dust` / `ether` / `ether_crystal` / `ether_ingot`; craft `ether_block` (5 strength, needs the correct tool).
- **Materials** — `blade`, `diamond_needle`, `gold_screw`, `vacuum_pipe`, `etherphilic_bowl`, `warden_heart`, `inactivated_ether` are crafting components; `inactivated_ether_block` and `smooth_inactivated_ether_block` (5 strength) are storage blocks.
- **`ether_creative`** is a creative-only ether resource; `logo` is a decorative/log item.

## Cheese

`cheese` is a snack (2 hunger, 1.0 saturation, always edible) that **clears all status effects** on consumption. It also exists as the `cheese_block` (0.5 strength, wool sound).
