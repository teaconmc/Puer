# Getting started

All Icarus Wings items live in the **Icarus Wings** creative tab (`locusazzurro_icaruswings:icaruswings`). The theme is «bees, feather and the sky» — a progression woven from feathers, wax and mythical materials.

## Core resources

- **Feathers** — `red_feather`, `blue_feather`, `green_feather`, `cyan_feather`, `gray_feather`, `golden_feather`, plus `feather_bunch` / `colored_feather_bunch` / `golden_feather_bunch` for crafting wings.
- **Wax & alloys** — `beeswax` (a food too) and the refined wax blocks, plus `steel_ingot`, `magma_ingot`, `purpur_ingot` (and their `*_mixture` stage).
- **Essences & gems** — `zephir_essence`, `nether_essence`, `void_essence`; `ultramarine_gem`, `sapphire_gem`, `blue_topaz_gem`.
- **Textiles** — `flax`, `linen`, `linen_string`, `wheat_grains`, `golden_string`, `golden_fleece`.

## Armour and early equipment

There are cloth/plant armours (`herbal_crown`, `feather_helmet`, `golden_feather_helmet`, `linen_cloak`, `linen_undergarment`, `golden_fleece_mantle`, `beeswax_boots`) and a `beeswax_block` / refined-wax building set. A `gold_upgrade_smithing_template` upgrades gear, and `reforged_netherite_ingot` / `synapse_alloy_ingot` open the late-game materials.
