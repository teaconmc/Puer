# Icarus Wings（伊卡洛斯之翼）

Icarus Wings (mod id `locusazzurro_icaruswings`, version `26.1.2-0.7.0`) — "A story about bees, feather and the sky. A testament to human ingenuity." Developed by LocusAzzurro (GPL-3.0) under the `org.mineplugin.locusazzurro.icaruswings` project, targeting Minecraft 26.1.2 on NeoForge (`26.1.2.36-beta`).

## What it offers

- **Wings & flight** — a full family of wearable wings (`feather_wings`, `colored_feather_wings`, `golden_feather_wings`, `paper_wings`, `magic_wings`, `flandre_magic_wings`) plus the high-tech Synapse wings (`ikaros_wings`, `nymph_wings`, `astraea_wings`, `chaos_wings`, `hiyori_wings`, `melan_wings`).
- **Materials & crafting** — feathers, wax, flax/linen, steel/magma/purpur ingots, essences and gems, all in the `icaruswings` creative tab.
- **Bees, mead & elysian farming** — beeswax blocks, glass/air jars, infused meads, honey cakes, golden wool, the golden ram and the elysian dimension blocks.
- **Combat & tech** — multiple spear tiers, the Artemis launcher, time-rift technology, fallen relics and transport cards.
