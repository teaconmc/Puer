# Farming, bees and the elysian

## Bees and wax

- **`beeswax`** is a food and a crafting material; refine it into `refined_beeswax_bar` and `glistering_refined_beeswax_bar`, then the refined wax building blocks (`refined_beeswax_block`, `_slab`, `_pillar`, `_stairs`, `chiseled_*`, `smooth_*`).
- **`glass_jar`** captures bees and other small things; **air jars** hold air types.
- `beeswax_boots` let you stand on honey-like surfaces.

## Mead

The `mead_pot` block and `amphora` storage make mead. Infused meads (`mead`, `zephir_infused_mead`, `nether_infused_mead`, `void_infused_mead`, `golden_apple_infused_mead`, `golden_apple_growth_infused_mead`, `herbs_infused_mead`) restore hunger with special effects.

## Crops and elysian blocks

- **`flax_seeds` / `flax` crop** grow flax → `linen`, `linen_string`.
- **`wheat_grains`** and `wheat_string` come from wheat; `grains_meal` is a bowl-keeping snack.
- The elysian set: `elysian_grass_block`, `elysian_soil`, `elysian_grass` — grass-like blocks themed to the mythic plains.

## Golden ram & honey

- **`golden_ram_spawn_egg`** summons the `golden_ram` mob (a golden-fleeced sheep); shear it for `golden_fleece` → `golden_string`, and craft `golden_wool_block` / `golden_wool_carpet` / `golden_fleece_mantle`.
- **`honey_cake`** is a placeable honey food block; `melon` is a custom fruit item.
