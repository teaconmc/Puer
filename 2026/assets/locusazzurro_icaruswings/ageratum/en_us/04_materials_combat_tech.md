# Materials, combat and tech

## Spears

Throwable spears in every tier: `wooden_spear`, `stone_spear`, `iron_spear`, `steel_spear`, `golden_spear`, `diamond_spear`, `netherite_spear`, `synapse_alloy_spear`. They use the `throwing` data component and the `spear` damage source.

## Artemis & time-rift tech

- **`artemis_launcher`** fires `artemis_missile` projectiles; transport cards `transport_card_artemis_homing` / `transport_card_artemis_scatter` control guidance.
- **Time rift** — `time_rift_generator`, `time_rift_charge`, the `time_rift` damage source and entities (`time_bomb`, `time_rift_particle`, `kayros_energy_blast`); `demeter` / `demeter_charge` disable time in an area.
- **Transport cards** — `transport_card_base`, `transport_card_chrono_explosion`, `transport_card_chrono_teleport`, `transport_card_masking_field`, `transport_card_interdiction_field`, `transport_card_treasure` use the `destination` data component.

## Fallen relics & Synapse gear

- **Fallen relics** — `fallen_relic_core/interface/offensive/defensive/propulsion`, restored and upgraded versions, decoded via `daedalus_manuscript` / `daedalus_decryptor`.
- **Synapse armour & tools** — `synapse_helmet/chestplate/leggings/boots`, `synapse_alloy_sword`, `steel_pickaxe`.
- **Effects** — `poison_immunity`, `sensory_masking`, `inevitability`, `interdiction`.

## Other

- **Greek fire** — a `greek_fire` fluid (`greek_fire_bucket`) and `greek_fire` block.
- **Discs & misc** — `disc_fallen_down`, `disc_ring_my_bell` (jukebox discs), `icon_badge`, plus custom enchantments, villager trades and the `icaruswings` config.
