# Furniture Plan

Furniture Plan is an alpha NeoForge furniture mod. The current `0.1.00` source targets Minecraft `26.1.2`, generates seating, surfaces, columns, and storage from many vanilla materials, and adds lighting, plant stands, and tableware that can display food and potions.

## Reading path

1. [Getting Started](01_getting_started): creative tabs, ID patterns, and the current way to obtain content.
2. [Seating and Tables](02_seating_and_tables): sitting, occupancy rules, and automatically connected tables.
3. [Lighting and Decor](03_lighting_and_decor): columns, table lamps, hanging lanterns, and three-position plant stands.
4. [Storage Furniture](04_storage): cabinets, cupboards, capacity, opening conditions, and automation.
5. [Tableware and Food](05_tableware): iron pots, plates, glasses, potions, and dismantling place settings.
6. [Catalog and Current Limitations](06_catalog_and_limitations): counts, material families, miscellaneous items, and unfinished features.

## Scale at a glance

The generated resources currently contain `2619` `furnitureplan` item definitions, including `108` material families, `1836` cabinet appearances, and `16` colored pot holders. The catalog is large, but interaction rules stay consistent by furniture type.

::: warning
The current source neither generates nor ships any recipes, and the recipe provider is empty. Read Getting Started for the available acquisition methods; this documentation does not reference nonexistent recipe IDs.
:::
