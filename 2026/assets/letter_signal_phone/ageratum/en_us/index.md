# Letter, Signal, Phone

**Letter, Signal, Phone** (namespace `letter_signal_phone`, version `1.0.0`, All Rights Reserved) is a communication mod for Minecraft 26.1.2 on NeoForge. It adds three parallel ways for players to reach each other across a world:

- **Letters and parcels** delivered through bound mailboxes, with a collectable stamp system.
- **Telegraph machines** that broadcast Morse-style signals on a frequency.
- **Phones** with registered numbers and real UDP voice calls.

Plus two flavour systems: **owls** with their nests, and **messages in a bottle** that drift in the sea.
