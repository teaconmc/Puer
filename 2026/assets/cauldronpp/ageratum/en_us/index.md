# Cauldron++

Cauldron++ is a mod that brings the Beta 1.9-pre2 brewing system back to modern Minecraft. Instead of using brewing stands, you brew potions directly in a special cauldron by adding water, materials, and ingredients, recreating the experimental potion mechanics from Minecraft's early days.
