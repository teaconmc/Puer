# The Obelisk Depths dimension

The Obelisk Depths (`obeliskdepths:obelisk_depths`) is a separate dimension generated with a fixed, single biome: the **Great Swamp** (`great_swamp`).

## A dark, endless cave

The dimension type is configured as an enclosed underground space:

- Fixed time and no sky light; the sky is black and there is a ceiling.
- Vertical range from Y -64 up to height 384.
- No ender-dragon fight; beds cannot be used to sleep and respawn anchors do not work here.
- Modular ambient mood sound, low ambient light, deep-black fog.

These settings mean you are always underground in near-total darkness, so bring light sources.

## The Great Swamp biome

The Great Swamp biome (`obeliskdepths:great_swamp`) is a damp, cool biome (temperature 0.8, downfall 0.9) with muted swamps water and foliage colours, dry-foliage tinting and a dark fog. Its terrain uses natural blocks — dirt, coarse dirt, grass block and mud — plus the great swamp taxodium (a bald-cypress-like tree) with logs, planks, leaves, vines, root tangle and vine bulbs.

In the shipped biome definition the spawn tables and features lists start out empty, so no mob or feature is generated by the biome itself.

## Dungeon content is data-driven

Dungeon layouts are loaded from data packs at server reload time (`dungeon_content` listener), so themes, rooms and corridors can be added or replaced without recompiling. The shipped **Great Swamp** theme provides structure pieces for:

- **Start** and **combat** rooms.
- A **boss** altar room.
- A **treasure** room (`obelisk_sanctum`).
- **Corridor** pieces: straight, corner, tee and cross.

During a run the mod keeps you inside your dungeon boundary and can surface a **Dungeon Raid** objective (kill count) and a boundary warning when you stray too far.
