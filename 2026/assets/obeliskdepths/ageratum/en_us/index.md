# Obelisk Depths

Obelisk Depths (ObeliskDepths) is a dungeon-exploration mod built around obelisks. Right-click an Obelisk, offer a tribute, and step through the portal it opens to enter the dark Obelisk Depths dimension: procedurally generated dungeon sites with corridors and themed rooms, loot, and reward progression.
