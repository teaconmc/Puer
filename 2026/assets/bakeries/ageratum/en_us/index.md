# Bakeries

**Bakeries** (mod id `bakeries`, by Renyigesai, 1-weibai-1, NeptuneTADASHI and zhifan) is a full baking overhaul: six custom workstations, 71 blocks, 65 items, and a **temperature system where getting the heat exactly right lets you eat even when you are not hungry**.

It is one of the largest content mods in this batch by resource count — 512 models and 299 textures — and it ships a complete Patchouli guide plus a **Literary Chinese (`lzh`) localization**.

## What makes it distinctive

**A real temperature mechanic.** The oven is not a timer — you set a temperature with the mouse wheel, and hitting the right band produces food with a **Perfect Temperature** entry. Such food carries a benefit no ordinary food has: it can be eaten at full hunger.

**Six purpose-built stations** rather than one machine that does everything: oven, blender, dough crafting table, flour sieve, bread knife and drink station each have their own recipe type.

**A Literary Chinese translation.** Beyond `en_us` and `zh_cn`, the mod ships `lzh` — and the renderings are inventive: a croissant becomes **朔月饢** ("new-moon bread"), a baguette **芳司棍饢**, the guide book **《行饢記》**.

::: tip
**Install Patchouli.** The mod says so itself — on login it prints *"[Bakeries] Install Patchouli to obtain the mod tutorial book"*. With 43 book files and a mechanic built on numeric temperature bands, the in-game guide is genuinely the primary reference. These pages cover the system and the source-level facts around it.
:::
