# Languages and Good to Know

## Three languages, including Literary Chinese

The mod is playable in **English**, **Simplified Chinese**, and — unusually — **Literary Chinese (文言文)**.

English and Simplified Chinese are both complete: everything you see in game is translated in either one.

## The Literary Chinese renderings are worth seeing

If you switch your language to Literary Chinese, the bakery vocabulary gets reinvented rather than transliterated:

| Item | English | Literary Chinese |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Oven | Oven | **焙爐** |
| Croissant | Croissant | **朔月饢** (new-moon bread) |
| Baguette | Baguette | **芳司棍饢** |
| Bagel | Bagel | **韌環饢** |
| Ciabatta | Ciabatta | **鞾饢** (boot bread) |
| Focaccia | Focaccia | **中爐饢** |
| Pineapple bun | Pineapple Bun | **鳳梨饢** |
| Eternal Baguette | Eternal Baguette | **亙久芳司棍饢** |
| Baking Guide | Baking Guide | **《行饢記》** |

Even the Perfect Temperature explanation is rewritten in classical style:

> **English**: Food with a Perfect Temperature entry can be eaten even when you are not hungry
> **文言文**: 全美火候之焙食者，飢滿亦可食之也。

::: warning
**Literary Chinese is partial.** A good number of items and screens still fall back to English while playing in it — it covers a portion of the mod rather than all of it.

Treat it as a flavourful alternative for a themed playthrough, not as a complete translation. English and Simplified Chinese are the two fully supported languages.
:::

## Things that are easy to miss

A short list of behaviours that surprise people, gathered in one place:

**Baking at the minimum temperature never gives Perfect Temperature.** The tooltip shows a floor, not the target band. See [The oven and Perfect Temperature](02_oven_and_perfect_temperature).

**The flour sieve goes in your off hand.** Material in the main hand. See [Tools and their quirks](04_tools_and_quirks).

**The mould needs a knife *and* a long press.** Neither alone releases it.

**The toaster starts from the left hand.**

**The Eternal Baguette is a weapon.** It knocks entities back and slows them, so do not leave it in a hotbar slot you press by accident.

**Crops get eaten by animals.** The mod's crops behave like vanilla ones in that respect, so fence your farm.

**Some food hurts you.** Raw peppers deal damage when eaten, and a few dishes carry their own damage types — the death message will name them specifically.

## Where to get help in game

The mod tells you on login to install **Patchouli**, and that advice is worth following: the **Baking Guide** book is where temperature bands, recipe chains and station usage are documented in detail. These pages cover the systems; the book covers the numbers.
