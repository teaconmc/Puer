# SuperPipeSlide

SuperPipeSlide (`superpipeslide`) adds invisible pipe networks that players ride by sliding along their centerline. You place anchors, connect them with line or curve tools, then walk into the pipe to be captured and launched through the network. Branch anchors, space and dimension folds, stations, routes and projection blocks turn the network into a full transport system.
