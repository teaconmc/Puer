# Getting Started

NekoPLUS! requires AnvilCraft 1.5.0 or newer. All content is added on top of AnvilCraft's blocks, machines and recipes.

## Creative Tab

The mod registers the creative tab `itemGroup.nekoplus.tab`, whose icon is the High Energy Laser. All NekoPLUS! items and blocks are grouped there.

## Titanium Alloy Materials

The base material chain consists of:

- `nekoplus:titanium_alloy_ingot`
- `nekoplus:titanium_alloy_nugget` (from ingots)
- `nekoplus:titanium_alloy_block` (storage block, with the usual conversions back)
- `nekoplus:titanium_alloy_anvil`

The ingot itself is produced by an AnvilCraft super-heating recipe:

<recipe id="nekoplus:titanium_alloy_ingot_superheating"/>

One item from the `#c:ingots/titanium` tag plus one iron ingot yields 2 titanium alloy ingots.

## Enhancement Modules

The mod registers the base item and a set of enhancement modules that are stored in the `nekoplus:enhancement_module` data component:

- `nekoplus:enhancement_module_base`
- `nekoplus:titanium_crystal_module`
- `nekoplus:hologram_projector_module`
- `nekoplus:mechanical_heart_module`
- `nekoplus:exoskeletal_leg_frame_module`

## Machines and Blocks

Registered blocks whose recipes are generated by the mod include:

- `nekoplus:battery` and `nekoplus:huge_battery`
- `nekoplus:particle_stabilizer`
- `nekoplus:high_energy_laser`
- `nekoplus:shulker_hatch`
- `nekoplus:programmable_logic_gate`
- `nekoplus:blast_crystal` (with `cracked` and `damaged` variants)
- `nekoplus:stellar_engine` and `nekoplus:tardis` multi-block structures

## Configuration and Commands

The mod registers a common config and a client inspection command. Details belong to their dedicated pages once released.
