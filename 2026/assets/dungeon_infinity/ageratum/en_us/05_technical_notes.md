# Good to Know

## ⚠️ Do not sleep in the maze

::: danger
**Beds explode in the maze dimension.** You cannot sleep, you cannot set your spawn point, and attempting it detonates the bed — exactly like the Nether.

**Set your respawn point before you go in.** There is no way to make a safe waypoint inside the maze, so plan your trips around returning rather than around resting.
:::

## Other ways the maze differs from the overworld

| Behaviour | In the maze |
|:---|:---|
| **Beds** | **Explode** — no sleeping, no spawn point |
| **Lava** | Flows fast, as in the Nether |
| **Raids** | Cannot be started |
| **Piglins** | Do not zombify, and portals do not spawn them |
| Ambient light | Dim but never fully dark |
| Weather | None |

## Nothing generates naturally in the maze

The maze has **no ore, no caves, and no natural mob spawns** of its own. Everything you meet was placed deliberately by the dungeon generator.

::: tip
**This is why the maze feels authored rather than random.** Do not go in expecting to mine for resources — there are none in the walls. Everything worth having is in rooms, chests and shops.

It also means the danger level is entirely designed. If a floor feels harder, that is intentional, not bad luck with spawns.
:::

## Nothing in this mod is craftable

There are **no recipes at all**. The keys, the map and the blocks come from the dungeon itself, from shops, or from creative mode.

So the Key of Tomb and Key of Access are things you **find or buy**, never things you make.

## ⚠️ Required companion mods

::: danger
**This mod cannot run alone.** It is part of a first-party chain and needs both:

- **Modular Golems**
- **Golem Dungeons**

plus several supporting libraries from the same author.

**A missing dependency causes a startup failure, not a degraded experience.** There is no graceful fallback — if the game will not launch after adding this mod, a missing companion mod is the first thing to check.

The good news is that they are all by the same author and versioned together, so installing the set as a group avoids the problem entirely.
:::

## Seven difficulty tiers, five themed layouts

The mod defines **seven tiers** of enemies and equipment — early, copper, stone, mineshaft, deepslate, sculk and deepest.

::: info
**But only five of them have their own structure sets.** The deeper two tiers raise the difficulty without introducing new room layouts, so you will recognise the architecture while the danger keeps climbing.

Worth knowing so you do not conclude the dungeon has stopped generating new content — it is the enemies that scale at that point, not the rooms. See [The maze](02_the_maze).
:::

## Language support

**English and Simplified Chinese are both complete and fully matched.** There is no translation debt here — a Chinese client shows proper names throughout.

## Redistribution
