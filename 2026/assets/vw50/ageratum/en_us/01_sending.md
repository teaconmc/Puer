# Getting the red envelope

## Obtaining the item

The mod registers a single item, `vw50:empty_red_envelope`. There is one shapeless recipe that yields four at once:

<recipe id="vw50:empty_red_envelope"/>

- 2 Paper
- 1 Red Dye
- → 4 Empty Red Envelope

## Opening the send menu

Hold the Empty Red Envelope in your hand and use it (right-click). A send menu opens with a single **gift slot** plus fields for a **title** (up to 20 characters) and the **number of players** (1 to 256). Anything you drop into the gift slot becomes the envelope's gift stack.

::: note
The whole one-slot stack is used as the gift. Decide the amount before you send — you cannot take part back out of a stack that has already been packed.
:::

## Sending

Press **Send** in the menu. If you are not in Creative mode, the envelope consumes:

- **1** Empty Red Envelope from your main or off hand, and
- the **full gift stack** you packed.

The envelope is then announced as a chat card. Spam protection applies: a normal (non-Game-Master) player can send only with the default 30-second cooldown unless an administrator raises or removes it (see [Commands](04_commands)).
