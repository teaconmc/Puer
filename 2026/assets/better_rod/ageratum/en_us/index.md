# Better Rod

Better Rod turns the **vanilla fishing rod** into a modular tool. It adds no rod of its own — instead your ordinary rod gains three slots, and you fit it with a **line**, a **hook** and an **accessory** to change what fishing does.

Fitted out, a rod stops being a way to pull up cod and becomes a weapon that poisons whatever the line touches, a grapple that teleports mobs to your feet, a machine that cooks its own catch, or a tool that fishes in lava.

## The three slots

| Slot | What it changes |
|:---|:---|
| Hook | what the bobber *is* — damage, lava resistance, potion effects, area pull |
| Line | what happens *along the line* — poison, weakness, elasticity, teleport |
| Accessory | what happens to the *catch* — bucketing, smelting, bite speed, luck |

Baits are separate: they are not fitted to the rod. You drop them in water to draw fish in.
