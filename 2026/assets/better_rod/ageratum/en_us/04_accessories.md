# Accessories

The accessory slot changes what happens to the **catch** rather than how you catch it. Four options, no overlap between them.

## Quick Clock — more bites

The straightforward one, and the one you will miss most once removed.

Every tick it advances the rod's internal timers:

- **Waiting for a bite: 2 ticks faster per tick**, so roughly three times the normal rate.
- **Waiting for the lure phase: 1 tick faster per tick**, roughly double.

Both are floored at 1, so the rod never skips a phase entirely — it just gets there much sooner. Lure enchantments stack with this; they reduce the base timer, and the clock then eats through what remains.

## Lucky Clover — Luck +5

Adds **+5 Luck** while the rod is **held in your main hand**.

That is a large number in fishing terms. Luck shifts the weighted pick away from junk and toward treasure, so a clover meaningfully changes what comes up — including in lava, where the same quality weighting applies. See [06 Lava fishing](06_lava_fishing).

Because the modifier lives on the rod and applies to the main hand, it stops the moment you put the rod away. It is a fishing bonus, not a permanent buff.

## Auto Smelter — cooked on arrival

Every item you reel in is run through the **vanilla smelting recipe** for it, if one exists, and arrives smelted.

Raw cod becomes cooked cod. Anything the furnace can process is processed. Anything with no smelting recipe passes through untouched, so nothing is lost by fitting it.

It costs no fuel and no time. Paired with the Blaze Hook — which smelts *dropped item stacks* it hooks — you have two independent free furnaces on one rod.

## Auto Filler — fish straight into buckets

Two behaviours, both about buckets:

**It buckets what you hook.** If the thing on your hook is a bucketable fish and you have a **water bucket anywhere in your inventory**, the bucket is converted to the matching fish bucket, the fish is removed from the world, and you get the pickup sound. It also grants the vanilla "filled a bucket" advancement.

**It buckets what you catch.** Caught loot is passed through the mod's four bucket-filling recipes, covering **cod, salmon, pufferfish and tropical fish** — the four vanilla bucketable species.

Two consequences worth planning for:

- **You must carry water buckets.** With none in your inventory the accessory does nothing at all; there is no warning.
- **The fish is consumed, not caught.** A bucketed fish is gone from the world and exists only as the bucket, so this is a collection tool rather than a food source. If you want food, fit the Auto Smelter instead.

## Choosing

| Goal | Accessory |
|:---|:---|
| Fish faster | Quick Clock |
| Better loot quality | Lucky Clover |
| Food without cooking | Auto Smelter |
| Collect live fish | Auto Filler |

Only one accessory fits at a time, and remember the removal order: the line comes off first, then the hook, then the accessory. Swapping accessories on a fully fitted rod means stripping all three. See [01 Getting started](01_getting_started).
