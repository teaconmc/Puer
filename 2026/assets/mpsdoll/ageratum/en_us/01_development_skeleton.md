# Current State of the Mod

::: danger
**There is nothing to do in game.** This mod currently adds no items, no blocks, no creative tab and no config file.

It exists as a starting point the author has not built on yet. If you installed it expecting doll content, that content does not exist in this build.
:::

## What you will see

| In game | What appears |
|:---|:---|
| Creative tab | **None** |
| Items or blocks | **None** |
| Config file | **None is created** |
| Mod list entry | The name, with placeholder text |

Nothing crashes and nothing misbehaves — there is simply no gameplay here yet.

## If you were looking for the doll

The doll itself has not been implemented. When it arrives, it will presumably come with its own items and mechanics, and these pages will change accordingly.

Until then there is genuinely nothing to test, configure or play with.

## For completeness

A few details, should you need them:

- **No Chinese translation ships**, so Chinese clients show short English placeholder names rather than anything localised.
- **The mod needs only NeoForge and Minecraft**, both required.
- **Adding it to a pack is harmless but pointless** until real content arrives.
