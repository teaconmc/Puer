# Characters

There are **32 characters**. Each is a separate identity with its own health, skills, bond track and wardrobe.

## Picking one

Open **Character Settings** and use the **Character Select** screen:

| Button | Does |
|:---|:---|
| **Use Character** | Play as them — shows **In Use** afterwards |
| **Return to Player** | Go back to being yourself |
| **Character Details** | Their full archive |
| **Locked** | You do not have them yet |

## Which ones you start with

**Five characters are available immediately**, their hint reading *"Unlocked by default"*:

- **Alana**
- **Fanny**
- **Komachi**
- **Mimi**
- **Parunan**

One more tells you how to get it:

- **LingLing** — *"Unlock LingLing through AstralCraft adventures."*

::: warning
**The remaining 26 characters do not state how to unlock them.** Their unlock hint exists as a field but is empty, so the game shows you a locked character with no instruction.

That is not something you can solve by playing more carefully — the information simply is not written yet. Treat the five default characters plus LingLing as the current roster, and see [What is finished and what is not](06_current_state).
:::

## The Character Details archive

Each character's archive has four sections:

| Section | Contents |
|:---|:---|
| **Profile** | Their background text |
| **Skills** | Active and passive, with cooldowns |
| **Upgrade** | Level progression |
| **Potential** | An unlockable enhancement — **only some characters have one** |

## Skills: two of them, four cooldowns

Every character has an **active** and a **passive** skill. The active one is bound to the *Use Character Skill* key.

What is easy to miss: **each skill can behave differently against monsters than against players**, and the cooldowns are listed separately.

::: tip
For example a character may have a 3-turn cooldown in PVE and a 3-turn cooldown in PVP — but there is no rule that the two match. **Check both numbers in Character Details** before you commit to a duel, because the skill you rely on while walking the board may not be ready as often in a fight against another player.
:::

## Potential: only four characters have one

The **Potential** tab describes an unlockable enhancement:

> *"This character has an unlockable potential."*
> *"After activation, this character's skill is enhanced."*

**Four characters currently have a potential described**: **Fen**, **Jill**, **Mimi** and **Z3000**.

::: info
Mimi is the useful one to note — she is **both unlocked by default and has a potential**, which makes her the character you can actually explore this system with from the start.
:::

## Bonds

Character Settings shows **Bond x/y** — a friendship track with a per-character maximum (commonly 6 levels).

::: warning
**The bond reward system is not finished.** The screen carries a placeholder that says so directly: it is reserved for friendship levels, gifts, contracts and contract rewards, with a note that rewards may later appear as separate cards or be tied to item costs.

So bonds display a number today, but the gifts-and-contracts layer around them is not implemented. Do not plan a session around farming bond rewards.
:::

## Outfits

There are **58 character skins** across **6 rarity tiers**. Character Settings has an animation preview with **Play** and **Pause**, so you can watch an outfit's animation before equipping it.

Skins are cosmetic — they change how your character looks, not what they do.

## Health varies by character

Each character declares their own base health — for example one has 9. **Characters are not statistically identical**, so switching character changes how much punishment you can absorb, not just your skill and appearance.
