# Your First Game

## What you need

Three things, all from the **Astral Craft** creative tab:

| Item | Role |
|:---|:---|
| `astral_craft:board_projector` | **Astral Board Projector** — sets up the board |
| `astral_craft:astral_dice` | **Astral Dice** — your movement roll |
| Hand cards | Your deck; `handcard_*` items |

Since nothing here is craftable, open the creative tab or use `/give`. On a server, an operator handing out a projector is the usual way to start a session.

## How a turn goes

1. **Roll the dice.** The Astral Dice decides how far you move.
2. **Move along the board.** The board is a path of squares, and some squares branch — you will sometimes choose which way to go.
3. **Resolve the square you land on.** Every square type does something different; [The board](02_the_board) lists all 22.
4. **Fight if you meet someone.** Landing on a monster, or running into another player, opens **Battle Preparation** — see [Cards and battles](03_cards_and_battles).
5. **Pass through Check Point** to complete a lap.

## Before you roll: set up your character

Open **Character Settings** and do three things:

- **Pick a character** with *Use Character*. The button *Return to Player* puts you back to normal.
- **Look at their skills.** Each character has an active and a passive skill, and the active one is on the *Use Character Skill* key.
- **Check the bond level.** Shown as *Bond x/y* — this is the progress track for that character.

::: info
**Skills have separate cooldowns for fighting monsters and fighting players.** A character's active skill lists a PVE cooldown and a PVP cooldown independently, so a skill that feels quick against monsters can be slower in a duel against another player. The Character Details screen shows both.
:::

## Choosing a card back

**Open Card Back Selection** lets you pick the back design of your cards. The screen says it plainly:

> *Click a card back to apply it. Other players will see it on your reveal animations.*

So it is **cosmetic but public** — it is what the table sees when you play a card, not something only you look at.

## Quick phrases

The fourth key opens **quick phrases** — canned lines for talking during a game without typing. In a party game where everyone is waiting on your turn, this is faster than chat.

## A practical first session

1. Give everyone a projector, dice and a starting hand.
2. Each player opens **Character Settings** and equips a character.
3. Set the board down with the projector.
4. Take turns rolling; resolve each square as you land.
5. Use **Use Character Skill** when the cooldown allows.

::: warning
**Read [What is finished and what is not](06_current_state) before planning a long campaign.** The board that ships with the mod is a small development loop rather than a full map, and most characters have no stated way to unlock them yet. Knowing that up front avoids hunting for content that is not there.
:::
