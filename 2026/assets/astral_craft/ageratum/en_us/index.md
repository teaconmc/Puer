# Astral Craft

**Astral Craft** is a party board game inside Minecraft. You roll dice, move around a board of themed squares, fight with a deck of cards, and play as one of **32 collectible characters** — each with skills, bonds and outfits.

Think of it as three games stacked together: **a board to walk**, **a deck to play**, and **a character to grow**.

## What you play with

| Piece | What it is for |
|:---|:---|
| **Astral Board Projector** | Sets up a board to play on |
| **Astral Dice** | Rolls your movement |
| **Hand cards** | Your deck — attack, defense and dozens of tricks |
| **Chips** | Equipment that raises your stats |
| **Characters** | Who you play as, with their own skills |

## The four keys

Everything you do outside the board is on a key:

| Key | Opens |
|:---|:---|
| **Open Character Settings** | Pick a character, check skills, bonds and outfits |
| **Use Character Skill** | Fire your active skill |
| **Open Card Back Selection** | Choose the card back other players see |
| **Open quick phrases** | Chat shortcuts during a game |

They all live under the **AstralCraft** category in Controls, so you can rebind them freely.

::: tip
**Set your character before your first game, not during it.** Character Settings is where you equip who you play as — and until you do, you are playing as your plain self. Two characters, **Alana** and **Fanny**, plus three others are unlocked from the start, so you always have something to pick.
:::

::: warning
**This mod has no crafting recipes at all.** No item in it can be crafted at a table, smelted, or obtained from a loot table — the board pieces, dice, cards and chips all come from Creative mode, `/give`, or from playing the board itself.

That is by design for a party game, but it means **a survival world will not hand you the projector**. See [Your first game](01_your_first_game).
:::
