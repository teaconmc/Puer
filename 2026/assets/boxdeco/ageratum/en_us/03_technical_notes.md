# Good to Know

## ⚠️ Breaking the railing destroys it

::: danger
**The glass railing cannot be crafted and does not drop itself when broken.**

There is exactly one block in this mod — a decorative glass railing — and it comes from the creative tab or `/give`. Once placed, **mining it gives you nothing back**, so every railing you break is gone.

**Place carefully, or keep spares in creative.** If you are decorating a large build, do not treat placed railings as recoverable.
:::

## The railing behaves like plain stone, not like glass

The block has no special material settings configured, so:

| Behaviour | What to expect |
|:---|:---|
| Mining speed | Like ordinary stone, not fast like glass |
| Best tool | No particular tool is favoured |
| Explosion resistance | Ordinary, not glass-like |
| Sound when walked on | Generic block sound, not glass |

It still *looks* right, and light passes through its thin shape correctly rather than casting a full-cube shadow. Only the physical feel differs from what the appearance suggests.

## Resource packs can change its glass

The railing's panel reuses the **vanilla light grey stained glass** texture; only the metal frame is this mod's own image.

::: info
**So a resource pack that retextured light grey stained glass will change your railings too.** That can be a feature — matching your pack automatically — or a surprise if you did not expect the link.
:::

## Chinese clients see English names

The mod has no Chinese translation. Because English is the fallback language, a Chinese client shows "BoxDeco" and "Glass Railing" as readable English rather than raw internal text.

Everything works in any language; it is simply untranslated beyond those two names.

## Nothing else is inside

This mod is genuinely one block and nothing more:

- No crafting recipe for that block
- No entities, no items beyond the railing itself
- No config options (the settings screen opens but is empty)
- No commands

If you were looking for more decoration pieces, this is the whole set in the current build.
