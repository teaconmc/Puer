# BoxDeco

BoxDeco is a single-block decoration mod. It adds a glass railing that automatically forms corners the way vanilla stairs do, giving you clean balcony and walkway edges without manual corner pieces.

Be aware before you start: the railing has **no crafting recipe and no drops**. See [01 Getting started](01_getting_started).
