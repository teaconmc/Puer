# Getting started

BoxDeco adds exactly one block, the Glass Railing (`boxdeco:glass_railing`). There is nothing to unlock and nothing to research.

## Obtaining it

The only supported way to get the railing is the creative inventory:

1. Open the creative inventory.
2. Find the **BoxDeco** tab — it sits immediately before the Combat tab.
3. The tab contains one entry: the Glass Railing.

There is **no crafting recipe** of any kind. The mod ships no recipe data, so the railing cannot be crafted, smelted, stonecut, or traded for. In survival without cheats it is unobtainable.

## It does not drop when broken

The mod ships **no loot table** for the block. Breaking a placed Glass Railing therefore yields **no item** — the block is simply destroyed and nothing is returned, in survival or creative alike.

Two practical consequences:

- **Every railing you place is consumed permanently.** Plan the layout before you build, because a mistake costs you the block.
- **Do not treat it as a reusable building material.** Copy finished sections with structure blocks or world-edit style tools rather than by breaking and replacing.

If you are building on a server, hand out the item with a give command rather than expecting players to recover it.

## Placing it

Place the railing like any block. Two things determine the state it takes:

- **Your facing at the moment of placement** sets the railing's direction.
- **Neighbouring railings** immediately decide whether this one becomes a straight section or a corner.

You do not choose the corner shape manually — it is computed for you. See [02 Shapes and corners](02_shapes_and_corners).

## What it is not

- **No block entity**, so it stores nothing and has no GUI.
- **No waterlogging.** It cannot be placed in a water source and hold the water.
- **No variants.** There is one railing, in one material and one colour.
