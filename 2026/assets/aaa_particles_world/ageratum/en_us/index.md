# AAA Particles: World

AAA Particles: World is a client-side visual add-on for **AAA Particles**. It adds no craftable content; instead, it automatically replaces or augments combat, explosion, dropped-item, and ambient effects.

## Reading path

1. [Getting started](01_getting_started): dependencies, activation, and a quick check.
2. [World effects](02_world_effects): critical hits, lightning, explosions, beams, fireballs, and fireflies.
3. [Client configuration](03_configuration): every toggle, concurrency cap, and default value.
4. [Compatibility and performance](04_compatibility_and_performance): particle levels, platform limits, and troubleshooting.

## At a glance

- The checked-out source is version `2.0.1-alpha.4` for Minecraft `26.1.2`.
- The mod requires `aaa_particles` version `2.1` or newer.
- Effects run automatically on the client. There are no dedicated keys, commands, items, blocks, or recipes.
- Every effect category can be disabled, and lightning, explosions, and loot beams also have concurrency limits.

::: tip
For a first run, read Getting Started and then safely observe a critical hit, lightning bolt, or explosion. If nothing appears at all, go directly to Compatibility and Performance.
:::
