# Redstone and advancements

## Random Transmitter

`random_transmitter` (随机发信器) is a small redstone utility block that emits random redstone pulses. Its crafting recipe:

<recipe id="voidinair:random_transmitter"/>

It is a cube in a casing, and each of its six sides can carry a **transmitter**:

- Right-click a side with another `random_transmitter` to attach a transmitter there.
- Use AnvilCraft's **Anvil Hammer** on the block to manipulate (move/remove) the transmitters.
- When the block is unpowered, each attached transmitter independently and randomly flips on/off roughly once per second; active transmitters output a full redstone signal (15) in their direction.
- When the block receives a redstone signal (`powered`), it stops the randomness and turns off the inactive transmitters.

Dropping the block returns the transmitters (up to six) as items.

## Advancements

Void in Air has a small advancement tree rooted at *Welcome to AnvilCraft: Void in Air*:

| Advancement | Requirement |
|:---|:---|
| Ready to fly in the Void | Obtain a Totem of Void |
| Galactic Bio-Comet | Consume a Totem of Void (trigger its death protection) |
| Cliffs & Caves | Obtain a Void Amulet |
| Null Point E- | Create a Void Fountain |
