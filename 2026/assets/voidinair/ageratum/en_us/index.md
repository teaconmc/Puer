# AnvilCraft: Void in Air

AnvilCraft: Void in Air (铁砧工艺：天猫行空, namespace `voidinair`) is an addon for AnvilCraft themed around the Void and black cats. It adds the Void Fountain that grows Void-decayed blocks, a family of amulets, the Black Cat anvil block and its head, a random redstone transmitter, and the Totem of Void. Everything appears in the **AnvilCraft: Void in Air** creative tab (`voidinair:tab`), whose icon is the Black Cat.

## At a glance

| Domain | Items / blocks | Notes |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Anvil block | `black_cat` | Workable anvil styled as a black cat; falling on it can scare out a real black cat |
| Production | `void_fountain` | Grows Void-decay blocks like a mineral fountain |
| Energy/redstone | `random_transmitter` | Randomly sends redstone pulses on each face |
| Decor | `black_cat_head`, `black_cat_wall_head` | Placeable and wall black-cat heads |
| Non-vanilla item | `totem_of_void` | Undying-totem upgrade for falling into the Void |
| Amulets | `void_amulet`, `snowflake_amulet`, `beehive_amulet`, `gold_amulet`, `dolphin_amulet`, `pumpkin_amulet`, `anvilcraft_amulet`, `black_cat_amulet` | Damage/entity immunity, utilities, and combined amulets |

The single creative tab is **AnvilCraft: Void in Air** (`voidinair:tab`), built before AnvilCraft's building-blocks tab, with the Black Cat as its icon.
