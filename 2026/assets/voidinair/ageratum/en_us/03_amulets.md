# Amulets

Void in Air adds a family of amulets that use AnvilCraft's amulet system. Each is a flat item you keep in your inventory or amulet slot. Several are combined into stronger amulets.

## Basic amulets

| Item | ID | Effect |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Void Amulet | `void_amulet` | Immune to Void damage (`amulet_valid/void`); when falling into the Void, grants Slow Falling and teleports you to the top of the Overworld. |
| Snowflake Amulet | `snowflake_amulet` | Immune to freeze damage; lets you walk on powder snow. |
| Beehive Amulet | `beehive_amulet` | Immune to bees/hive inhabitants; lets you safely collect honeycomb and honey; bee mobs stop targeting/attacking you. |
| Gold Amulet | `gold_amulet` | Piglins view you as wearing gold armor. |
| Dolphin Amulet | `dolphin_amulet` | Grants Dolphin's Grace III and makes Guardians less likely to notice you. |
| Pumpkin Amulet | `pumpkin_amulet` | Puts a virtual Carved Pumpkin on your head. |

Each basic amulet is made from a Silver Ingot plus theme materials in a **Jewel Crafting** recipe (AnvilCraft's jewel-crafting table). For example, the Snowflake Amulet needs Silver Ingots and three Powder Snow Buckets, the Beehive Amulet needs Honeycombs and Honey Blocks, the Dolphin Amulet needs Prismarine Shards and Prismarine Crystals, and the Pumpkin Amulet needs Pumpkin Seeds and Carved Pumpkins.

The Void Amulet additionally carries a death-protection component (Regeneration, Absorption, Fire Resistance and Slow Falling, plus a save-from-the-Void teleport and no-shrinking), like a stronger Totem of Undying.

## AnvilCraft Amulet

`anvilcraft_amulet` (砧艺护符) combines the four base AnvilCraft amulets (**Abnormal**, **Anvil**, **Gem** and **Nature**) in one item. It is made in a four-to-one smithing recipe using the four amulets, a Transcendium Block and the `four_to_one_smithing_template`. The tooltip says it *"includes all AnvilCraft's amulet effects (except Comrade Amulet)".*

## Black Cat Amulet

`black_cat_amulet` (黑猫护符) is the ultimate amulet — it acts as **all** Void-in-Air amulet effects (Void, Snowflake, Beehive, Gold, Dolphin, Pumpkin) plus the **AnvilCraft Amulet** (and acts as a Comrade/Black Cat amulet). It is made in an eight-to-one smithing recipe from the six Void-in-Air amulets, the AnvilCraft Amulet and AnvilCraft's Comrade Amulet, with a Transcendium Block and the `eight_to_one_smithing_template`.

Its special quirk: right-click while holding it to **sign** your name onto it. Once signed, damage dealt *by you* to a wearer of that same black cat amulet is ignored — a comrade-protection effect. The tooltip lists every signed player by name.
