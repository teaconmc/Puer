# Getting started

Void in Air is an AnvilCraft addon, so its content sits next to the base mod. All of it is gathered in the **AnvilCraft: Void in Air** creative tab (`voidinair:tab`), whose icon is the Black Cat.

## Black Cat

`black_cat` (黑猫砧) is an anvil-type block that works like a normal anvil (right-click opens the anvil screen). Its crafting recipe uses Void Matter and Void Matter blocks:

<recipe id="voidinair:black_cat"/>

It is a falling block. Like a llama, if it is dropped onto the ground after falling more than one block, there is a 1% chance it is "scared": the block breaks and spawns a real pure-black cat (with a 5% chance it is a kitten) with a cat sound.

## Black Cat Head

`black_cat_head` is a placeable black-cat head and `black_cat_wall_head` is its wall-mounted variant. They are decorative head blocks rendered with a custom `void` skull type. You can wear the head item on your head.

## Totem of Void

`totem_of_void` (虚空图腾) is an undying-totem upgrade with a Void theme. On death it acts as a totem of undying: it clears status effects, grants Regeneration II, Absorption II, Fire Resistance and Slow Falling, and — if you fell into the Void — teleports you to the maximum build height (scaled x/y) in the Overworld instead of dying.
