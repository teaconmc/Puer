# AnvilCraft: Extra Power

AnvilCraft:Extra Power (namespace `anvilcraftextrapower`) is an addon for the AnvilCraft mod. It adds new power generation, energy storage, magnetism, and combustion materials on top of the base mod's item and energy systems. Everything registers through the `anvilcraftextrapower` creative tab, whose icon is the AnvilCraft capacitor.

## Reading order

- [01 Getting started](01_getting_started.md) — the creative tab, the new materials (magnesium, sulfur, coal powder, acids), and battery items.
- [02 Power and nuclear](02_power_and_nuclear.md) — solar panels, the nuclear collector, uranium rods, capacitors, and the nuclear bomb.
- [03 Magnetism](03_magnetism.md) — the electromagnet, the magnetic display stand, and the llama anvil.
- [04 Burning blocks and decor](04_burning_and_decor.md) — burning coal and magnesium, ashes and magnesium oxide, and the sign/display decoration blocks.

## At a glance

| Area | Items & blocks | Notes |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Materials | `magnesium_ingot`, `magnesium_nugget`, `magnesium_block`, `sulfur`, `sulfur_block`, `coal_powder`, `ashes`, `magnesium_oxide` | New smelting/material items with tags (`ingots/magnesium`, `nuggets/magnesium`, `sulfur`, `dust/coal`, `storage_blocks/magnesium`, `storage_blocks/sulfur`) |
| Energy storage | `lead_acid_battery`, `lead_acid_battery_empty`, `multiphase_capacitor`, `multiphase_capacitor_empty`, `potato_battery`, `flashing_potato_battery` | All tagged `capacitor` |
| Power generation | `solar_panel`, `nuclear_collector`, `uranium_rod`, `burning_coal_block`, `burning_magnesium_block` | Heat sources and an AnvilCraft power producer |
| Magnetism | `electromagnet`, `magnetic_display_stand`, `llama_anvil` | The electromagnet is tagged `MAGNET`; the llama anvil is `NON_MAGNETIC` and an `anvil` |
| Weapons / hazards | `nuclear_bomb`, `mushroom_cloud`, `frost_controller`, `pedometer`, `anvil_projector` | Nuclear explosion damage type `anvilcraftextrapower:nuclear_explosion` |
| Decor | `light`, `earth`, `display_mushroomcloud`, ten `sign_*` blocks | Creative/display blocks and warning signposts |

The only creative tab is **AnvilCraft:Extra Power** (`anvilcraftextrapower:anvilcraftextrapower_tab`), with the AnvilCraft capacitor as its icon.
