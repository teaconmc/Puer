# Getting started

## The two starting tools

The mod registers no commands, and its first steps are about crafting two tools and digging up a seed.

### Grass Shear (`harvestheritage:grass_shear`)

Craft the Grass Shear from a Shears and two Sticks:

<recipe id="harvestheritage:grass_shear"/>

- 1 Shears
- 2 Stick
- → 1 Grass Shear

The Grass Shear destroys tagged grass blocks (`harvestheritage:can_shear` — short grass, tall grass, ferns and their dry variants) with a **7% chance** to drop an **Unknown Seed**. It is also what gets a seed packet back off a mature crop (see [Crop stands](04_crop_stands)).

### Magnifying Glass (`harvestheritage:magnifying_glass`)

Craft the Magnifying Glass from Glass Panes, an Amethyst Shard and a Stick:

<recipe id="harvestheritage:magnifying_glass"/>

- 3 Glass Pane
- 1 Amethyst Shard
- 1 Stick
- → 1 Magnifying Glass

The Magnifying Glass can be **worn on the head** (it is equippable in the helmet slot) or held in either hand. It is used to identify dropped items — most importantly Unknown Seeds (see [Identifying seeds](02_seed_identification)).

::: note
While you are holding or wearing a Magnifying Glass, a center overlay shows the seed, growth stage, speed and output when you look at a planted Crop Stand or a Seed Packet (see [Crop stands](04_crop_stands)).
:::

## Your first Unknown Seed

1. Craft the Grass Shear.
2. Break short or tall grass (or ferns) with it.
3. On a 7% chance per block, an **Unknown Seed** drops.

An Unknown Seed carries no hint of what it will become; you have to identify it before you can plant it.
