# Beecrasy

Beecrasy is built around portable bees, genetic breeding, hive production, and honey machinery. The current systems are extensive, but world generation and some late-game content are not connected yet. Read Current State before starting a survival playthrough.

## Reading order

1. [Current state and entry requirements](01_current_state): learn what survival can currently obtain.
2. [Bees and wild nests](02_bees_and_nests): meet queens, drones, larvae, and the Butterfly Net.
3. [Skeps and hives](03_skep_and_hive): understand slots, operating modes, and environmental stalls.
4. [Breeding and lifecycle](04_breeding_lifecycle): follow mating, eggs, larvae, and products.
5. [Genetics and sequencing](05_genetics_and_sequencing): use handheld and block sequencers.
6. [Biotopes and mutations](06_biotopes_and_mutations): understand environment checks and gene changes.
7. [Incense and parameters](07_incense_and_parameters): temporarily adjust colony performance.
8. [Honey Press](08_honey_press): press manually and extract honey fluid.
9. [Bee mail delivery](09_mail_delivery): pack attachments, pay postage, and receive mail.
10. [Beedi music](10_beedi_music): use Buzzers, Beediboxes, and records.
11. [Bee City](11_bee_city): see what currently works and what does not.
12. [Flowers and resources](12_flowers_and_resources): comb materials, flowers, and dye recipes.
13. [Configuration and known issues](13_configuration_and_issues): defaults, debug access, and workarounds.

::: warning
The current code registers no natural placement for bee nests or starter bees. Unless the pack supplies another entry route, the first bee or nest must come from Creative mode or commands.
:::
