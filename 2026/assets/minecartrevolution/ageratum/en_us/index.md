# MinecartRevolution（矿车革命）

MinecartRevolution (mod id `minecartrevolution`, version 1.0.0) — "More minecarts, more fun!" — adds a large family of minecart variants and a transformation system that lets you turn blocks and items into working minecarts. It is developed by Ryan100C, Liuyue_awa, Mulatram_UwU and Yakon0705 (MIT license) and targets Minecraft 26.1.2 on NeoForge (`26.1.2.75`).

## What it does

- **Wrench & transforms** — the `minecartrevolution:wrench` and the `@MinecartMapper` / `MinecartTransformManager` open a block-to-minecart transformation pipeline and configure minecart behaviours.
- **A broad minecart catalogue** — container minecarts (barrel, chest, copper chest, trapped chest, shulker, ender chest, dispenser), working-block minecarts (anvil, beacon, enchanting table, crafting table, smithing table, stonecutter, loom, cartography table, grindstone, jukebox), fluid minecarts (water, lava, portal), redstone minecarts (redstone, repeater, pressure plates, piston) and more, each with a `minecart_<block>` item.
- **Minecart chains** — link minecarts with chains (`MinecartChainManager`, `chain` entity) to pull a whole convoy.
- **Utility & fun** — magnet (lodestone), picker, sofa, wool, scaffold, mob head, door/trapdoor minecarts, plus special ones like the beacon and portal minecarts.
