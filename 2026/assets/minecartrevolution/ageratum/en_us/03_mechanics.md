# Mechanics

## Minecart chains

`MinecartChainManager` and the `minecartrevolution:chain` entity link minecarts into a convoy so a leading cart pulls the rest (`chainEntityTypeSupplier` is wired at setup). This is the mod's flagship convoy mechanic.

## Portals

Portal minecarts can carry a player or block through dimensions: `minecart_nether_portal` teleports to the Nether, `minecart_end_portal` to the End, with `PortalMinecartStorage` keeping portal-link state and `EnderPortalShakePacket` for the client shake. The `PortalMinecartTeleportCriterion` / `BeaconActivatedCriterion` advancement triggers fire on these accomplishments.

## Redstone & power

Power minecarts emit steady or directional redstone power as they move; pressure-plate and piston minecarts interact with the rail network.

## Advancement triggers

Progress is tracked by many custom criteria, including `BeaconActivatedCriterion`, `BabelCriterion` / `BabelTowerCriterion` (the "babel" scramble gamemode, `BabelScramblePacket`), `BlockCartCraftedCriterion`, `DjangoUnchainedCriterion`, `HoneyMinecartStuckCriterion`, `MovingOnJukeboxCartCriterion`, `NoGravityCriterion`, `SofaAwayCriterion`, `SpongeAbsorbedCriterion`. Recipes are generated by `MRRecipeProvider` (common and server datagen).

## Misc

- `minecart_sponge` / `wet_sponge` absorb nearby water over `SpongeMinecartEntity.ABSORB_RADIUS` up to `ABSORB_LIMIT`.
- Sponge, honey and other carts trigger their own criteria.
- Dynamic client lighting (`DynamicLightsSpread` / `DynamicLightsStorage`) and camera shake (`CameraShakeManager`) add polish.
