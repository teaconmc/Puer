# Playing With Other Mods

This mod needs nothing but NeoForge and Minecraft, yet it behaves better alongside five others. Nothing here is required — each is a bonus if you happen to have it.

## Seasonal mods: your grapes join their calendar

Two different seasonal mods are supported, each on its own terms:

**SereneSeasons** — the fruit and the vine belong to different seasons:

| Plant | Season |
|:---|:---|
| Grape crop | **Autumn** |
| Grapevine trellis | **Spring** |

**Ecliptic Seasons** — all six growing blocks are registered as **average-moisture crops**, covering the plain, ice and gold varieties of both crop and trellis.

::: tip
**You can run both at once.** They ask different questions — SereneSeasons asks "which season?", Ecliptic Seasons asks "how much moisture?" — and this mod answers each in its own language rather than forcing one classification.

Ecliptic Seasons is another entry in this same competition, so if you are playing the competition pack, your grapes will slot into its moisture system correctly with no setup.
:::

## Diet: grapes count as fruit

All four grape varieties — plain, ice, gold and green — register as fruit in **Diet**, so they contribute to that mod's nutrition groups without you configuring anything.

## Carry On: tavern blocks are deliberately locked down

::: info
**If you use Carry On and cannot pick up a tavern block, that is intended.**

Carry On normally lets you lift and relocate block entities wholesale. This mod opts its own blocks out — the sofas, the sandwich board, the whiskey block, the paintings, the grape crops and more.

For a tavern that is the right call: otherwise a visitor could walk off with your fermenting barrel or your furniture. Break and replace them the normal way instead.
:::

## Ponder: four animated tutorials

If you install **Ponder**, this mod provides four in-game animated scenes:

| Scene | Shows |
|:---|:---|
| **Barrel** | How the barrel works |
| **Pressing Tub** | How pressing works |
| **Grapevine planting** | Planting grapes |
| **Grapevine wild generation** | How grapes occur naturally |

::: tip
**These four scenes are this mod's only visual documentation.** If you want to *see* how barrels attach in a stack, or how wild grapes generate, installing Ponder is how — the animations show it far better than text.

Without Ponder everything still works; you simply do not get the tutorials.
:::

## Vanilla extras that need no mods at all

Two things work out of the box through vanilla systems:

| Item | Behaviour |
|:---|:---|
| **Grapevine** | Burns as fuel — **200 ticks**, about the same as a plank |
| Grape, gold grape, green grape | **Compostable at 50%** — and **villagers will use them** |
| Grapevine | Compostable at 25% |

::: info
`can_villager_compost` being true on the fruits means **a village composter will consume your grapes**. Worth knowing if you farm near a village and wonder where the harvest went.
:::

## Which mods are worth adding

| Mod | What you gain |
|:---|:---|
| **Ponder** | Four animated tutorials — the biggest quality-of-life gain |
| SereneSeasons *or* Ecliptic Seasons | Grapes obey seasons or moisture |
| Diet | Grapes count toward nutrition |
| Carry On | Not needed, but respected if present |
| Jade | Block tooltips work normally |
