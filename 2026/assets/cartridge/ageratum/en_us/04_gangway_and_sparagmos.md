# Gangway and sparagmos

## Gangway

**`gangway`** (舷梯) is a deployable bridging tool. `GangwayHandler` / `GangwayInputHandler` build the gangway from your facing direction; a beam visual is projected (`GangwayBeamRenderer`, `GangwayRenderer`, `GangwayPlayerRenderer`, `GangwayHud`) with `GangwayInputPacket` / `GangwayRenderPacket` syncing the client, and `GangwayItemRenderer` renders the held item.

## Sparagmos

**`sparagmos`** (also known as the 撕裂之枪) is a spear item. It's registered like the other tools and sits in the `cartridge` creative tab.
