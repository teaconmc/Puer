# Effects, Combat and Compatibility

## Two status effects

| Effect | What it is |
|:---|:---|
| **Blessing** | A beneficial effect |
| **Curse** | A harmful effect, with its own visuals — blood, confusion, illusions, vomiting and explosion events |

The curse is the more elaborate of the two: it does not just tick damage but produces visible, unsettling effects on the affected player.

## Combat stance

There is a **toggleable combat mode** with its own keybind. Toggling it switches you into a fighting stance and blocks other inputs while active.

::: tip
If your inputs seem unresponsive, check whether combat mode is on — it deliberately intercepts input while you are in stance.
:::

## Which mods it works alongside

| Mod | What it adds |
|:---|:---|
| **Carry On** | Carried hollows render correctly when picked up |
| **Touhou Little Maid** | Maids interact with this mod's content |
| **Curios** | Items can occupy curio slots |
| **GeckoLib** | Animated models for this mod's creatures |

All of these are optional. Without them the mod still works; only those specific touches are missing.

## Small quality-of-life tweaks

The mod also adjusts two interface details for its own use: how effects display in your inventory, and how the mod list renders its own entry.
