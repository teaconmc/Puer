# Cartridge（来自深渊：弹药包）

Cartridge (mod id `cartridge`, version `1.1.2.0`) is a *Made in Abyss*-themed NeoForge mod developed by Chlamydomonos (MIT) under `xyz.chlamydomonos.cartridge`, for Minecraft 26.1.2 / NeoForge (`26.1.2.68-beta`), built with Kotlin for Forge (KFF 6.2.0). It adds the **cartridge** (弹药包) transformation, deep-world tools and several quality-of-life systems.

## What it offers

- **Cartridge & backpack** — turn your `backpack` into a `cartridge` on the `surgery_table`, gaining abyss-flavoured benefits (and a matching death message).
- **Hollow** — the `hollow` mob with the `hollow_randomizer`, plus the carried-hollow rendering with Carry On integration.
- **Abyss editing** — the `abyss_create_/abyss_remove_` tools (levels 1–6) to shape abyss terrain in the world.
- **Misc tools** — the `gangway` and the `sparagmos` spear, plus `blessing` / `curse` effects, a combat mode and many integrations.
