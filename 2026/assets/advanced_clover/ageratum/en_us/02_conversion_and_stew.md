# Conversion and Suspicious Stew

<item id="advanced_clover:four_leaf_clover"/>

## Clover Conversion

<recipe id="advanced_clover:four_leaf_clover"/>

Four Clovers craft three Four-leaf Clovers.

<recipe id="advanced_clover:three_leaf_clover"/>

Three Four-leaf Clovers convert back into four Clovers. The recipes are exact inverses and neither create nor lose plants.

## Suspicious Stew

<recipe id="advanced_clover:suspicious_stew_from_three_leaf_clover"/>

A Clover combined with a bowl, red mushroom, and brown mushroom creates Suspicious Stew that grants `9` seconds of Bad Luck.

<recipe id="advanced_clover:suspicious_stew_from_four_leaf_clover"/>

Using Four-leaf Clover instead creates Suspicious Stew that grants `12` seconds of Luck. Both outputs are vanilla Suspicious Stew with their effect stored in the result component.

::: info
The Four-leaf Clover tooltip, "Craft with other items for...", points to the dynamic Magic Clover recipe, not only the conversions and stews on this page.
:::
