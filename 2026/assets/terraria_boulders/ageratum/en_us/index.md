# TerrariaBoulders（泰拉巨石）

TerrariaBoulders (mod id `terraria_boulders`, version 1.0) — Terraria-style boulders. Developed by XiaoJin and Txt Text (MIT license) under the `org.confluence.terraria_boulders` project, targeting Minecraft 26.1.2 on NeoForge (`26.1.2.73`), with optional Jade and JEI integration.

## What it does

- **Boulder types** — a family of rolling boulder blocks/entities: the classic `boulder`, `oak_log_boulder`, `follower_boulder`, `explode_boulder`, `rolling_cactus_boulder`, `bouncy_boulder`, `ghoulder`, `lava_boulder`, `spider_boulder`, `rainbow_boulder`, `snow_boulder`, `camouflaged_boulder` and `giant_boulder`.
- **Boulder cannon** — the `boulder_cannon` block with a rider seat (`CannonSeatEntity`) that launches boulders using the Terraria-style `TerraStyleExplosion`.
- **Tools & items** — the `boulder_glove` (handles/throws boulders, stacks to 1), a `camouflaged_boulder` that can be locked/unlocked, and `boulder_bread` (a food item that also serves as a block).
- **Effects & sounds** — custom status effects (e.g. `choking` from `boulder_bread`) and custom sounds/explosions.
