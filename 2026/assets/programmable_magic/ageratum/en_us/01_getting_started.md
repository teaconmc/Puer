# Getting started

All main content sits in the **`programmable_magic`** creative tab (`main`).

## Wands

- **`rg_alloy_wand`** — the main magic wand (stacks to 1). It carries a mana pool (1000) and recharge rate (5), and opens the `wand_menu` GUI where you load spells.
- **`wand_item_placeholder`** — a placeholder item usable to bind a wand (see the `bind_wand_item_placeholder` recipe serializer).

## Packed spells and tools

- **`packed_spell`** (stacks to 64) — a bound/packed spell item produced from the wand's composition.

## Getting a wand ready

1. Obtain `rg_alloy_wand`.
2. Open the wand GUI (`wand_menu`) and insert spell tokens.
3. Charge the wand through the mana network (see [Mana network and machines](04_mananet_and_machines)).
4. Fire spells in the world.
