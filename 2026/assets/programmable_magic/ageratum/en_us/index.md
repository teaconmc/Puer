# Programmable Magic（可编程魔法）

Programmable Magic (mod id `programmable_magic`, version `0.1.3`) is a programmable-magic tech mod by CreepeBucket (GNU GPL 3.0, `org.creepebucket.programmable_magic`) for Minecraft 26.1.2 / NeoForge (`26.1.2.66-beta`), with JEI and GeckoLib integration. You assemble **spells out of stackable spell tokens**, bind them into a **wand**, and power it through a **mana network**.

## What it offers

- **Wands** — `rg_alloy_wand` (1000 mana, charge 5) with a full GUI; `wand_item_placeholder`; plugins for storage, release, supply, packing and theming.
- **Programmable spells** — dozens of `spell_display_*` tokens: numbers, math, vectors, control flow, triggers and world/entity effects, fired as `spell_entity` projectiles.
- **Mana network** — `basic_mana_connector` nodes and the `NetworkManaManager`; machines `solar_panel`, `wind_turbine`, `dummy_block`.
- **Commands & JEI** — the `/prm` command and full JEI integration.
