# Our Thoughts Are One（我们意念合一）

Our Thoughts Are One (mod id `ourthoughtsareone`, version `0.0.1`) is a cooperative-experience mod by WhiseNT (MIT, `com.whisent.ourthoughtsareone`) for Minecraft 26.1 / NeoForge (`26.1.0.19-beta`). "Our thoughts are one" — it lets players link minds: fly together through a **flight link** and project a shared point of view through a **mental projection**.

## What it offers

- **Flight link** — the `flight_core` lets one player invite teammates to fly as one (`flight_link`), syncing flight between players.
- **Mental projection** — the `projection_core` places a `projection_anchor` that projects an idea/target to the group (`ProjectionManager`).
- Two items, one creative tab (`ourthoughtsareone`), client rendering and config.
