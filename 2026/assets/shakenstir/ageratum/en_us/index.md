# Shake n Stir

**Shake n Stir** — a NeoForge bartending mod for Minecraft 26.1.2 that lets you run your own bar. Hunt down spirits and botanicals, shake, stir and distil custom cocktails, pour them into glassware for the in-game **Bartender** to serve — then deal with the effects of drinking a little too much.

## Reading Guide

- [Getting Started](01_getting_started) — set up a bar, hire a Bartender, gather spirits and ingredients, and make your first drinks.

Drink responsibly: sipping too many cocktails applies the `drunk` effect and friends; keep `sobering_tea` around. The **F** key opens the in-game menu.
