# Skyward Dive

Skyward Dive uses the Titan Armor to provide group launches and gliding without elytra. This guide follows the current source of `skywarddive` 1.0.0.

## Reading Order

- [Titan Armor](01_titan_armor): the only current item, how to obtain it, and its equipment properties.
- [Starting a Dive](02_starting_a_dive): activation checks, food cost, and the waiting phase.
- [Group Launch](03_group_launch): joining, formation behavior, and leaving a group.
- [Kidnap Dive](04_kidnap_dive): the range and limits for carrying players and creatures.
- [Gliding and Landing](05_gliding_and_landing): ascent, the gliding effect, and ending a session.
- [Targeting and Waypoints](06_targeting_and_waypoints): cosmetic lock-on, death points, and spawn points.
- [Configuration and Limits](07_configuration_and_limits): common parameters, client options, and current boundaries.

## Basic Flow

1. Equip the Titan Armor in the chest slot.
2. Have at least the configured food level, which defaults to 6 points.
3. Press `V` for a normal dive, then wait for teammates to right-click and join.
4. Let the countdown finish or have the initiator press jump.
5. The group ascends, then starts formation gliding in the initiator's facing direction.

::: warning
The mod provides no crafting recipe for the Titan Armor. In the current version it must come from Creative mode, a command, or an acquisition method added by a modpack.
:::
