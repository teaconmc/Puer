# Titan Armor

<item id="skywarddive:titan_armor"/>

## The Only Current Item

Titan Armor is the only item currently registered by `skywarddive`, and it is required to initiate both a normal dive and a Kidnap Dive. It must be equipped in the chest slot; holding it or carrying it in the inventory does not pass the server check.

The item uses diamond chestplate material and armor properties. It has a custom appearance, the dropped item is fire resistant, and taking damage does not damage this equipment. Fire resistance here is an item property and does not give the wearer the Fire Resistance effect.

## Obtaining It

There is no recipe JSON. The Skyward Dive Creative tab contains only this item. Survival progression therefore needs an administrator item command or a recipe, loot source, or quest reward supplied by a modpack.

## Who Needs Armor

- The initiator must wear Titan Armor and meet the food requirement.
- A player who right-clicks to join needs no armor and pays no food.
- A player pulled into a Kidnap Dive also needs no armor.
- Carried non-player creatures have no equipment or food check.

Titan Armor only authorizes session creation. Once gliding begins, flight without elytra is maintained by the `skywarddive:sky_gliding` effect.
