# Shadows And Petals

**织影落花** — a NeoForge mod for Minecraft 26.1.2 that brings a Japanese-style garden and interior-decorating toolkit to the game. Shape stone into multi-block rockeries, rake gravel into kare-sansui sand patterns, build with three custom wood families, and furnish rooms with irori hearths, lamps, wind chimes, copper teapots and more.

- [Getting Started](01_getting_started) — carve your first rockery, rake your first samon, and start building.

## Quick Reference

| Category | Content |
|:---|:---|
| Mod id | `shadowsandpetals` |
| Creative tabs | `Shadows & Petals` (main), `Shadows & Petals: Nature`, `Shadows & Petals: Agriculture` |
| Tools | `hammer` (carve stone into rockeries), `chisel` (off-hand carving aid), `harrow` (rake gravel into samon) |
| Materials | `bauxite_ore` / `deepslate_bauxite_ore`, `raw_bauxite`, `aluminum_ingot`, `aluminum_block`, ingot piles |
| Wood families | Sakura (樱花木), Maple (枫木), Ginkgo (银杏木) — each with logs, planks, posts, stairs, slabs, fences, saplings, leaves and hedges |
| Garden blocks | `rockery` stone gardens, `samon` sand patterns, `shishi_odoshi` (bamboo water hammer), `orange_tree` |
| Furnishings | `irori` hearth (with a container screen), lamps, `wind_chime`, `copper_teapot`, seats |
| Entities / menus | `seat` (invisible sit entity) / `irori` menu |
