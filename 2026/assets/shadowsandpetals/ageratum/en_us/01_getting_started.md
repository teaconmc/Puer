# Getting Started

This page walks you through the first steps with Shadows And Petals: gathering materials, carving a rockery, raking a samon, and raising your first trees.

## Obtaining the Tools

Three tools open up the core mechanics, all found in the `Shadows & Petals` creative tab (or crafted in survival):

- **Hammer** — craft 3 `aluminum_ingot` around a stick (`AAA / ASA / _S_`). Hold it in your main hand to carve connected stone into rockeries.
- **Chisel** — craft 2 `aluminum_ingot` stacked (`A / A`). Hold it in your **off hand** while carving.
- **Harrow** — craft 3 `bamboo` (`BBB / _B_ / _B_`). Rake gravel into kare-sansui sand patterns.

Aluminum starts from bauxite: mine `bauxite_ore` (or `deepslate_bauxite_ore`), obtain `raw_bauxite`, then smelt or blast it into `aluminum_ingot`.

## Carving a Rockery (Stone Garden)

1. Put the **Chisel in your off hand** and the **Hammer in your main hand**.
2. Look at a block of *Stone* and hold right-click.
3. When the strike completes, the largest matching multi-block rockery is carved out of the connected stone (`rockery_<w>_<h>_<d>` with sizes up to 4×4×4). Chisel in hand is required for the carving to work.

## Raking a Samon (Dry Garden)

- Right-click **Gravel** with the Harrow to rake it into a `samon` block that connects to nearby samon.
- Right-click the **top of a samon** to cycle between straight and corner rake patterns.

## Planting Sakura, Maple and Ginkgo

Each wood family comes with its own sapling, leaves (with falling-leaf particles), and a full 18-block set: log, stripped log, wood, stripped wood, planks, posts, stairs, slabs, fence, fence gate, pressure plate, button, sapling, leaves and hedge. Place a `sakura_sapling` / `maple_sapling` / `ginkgo_sapling` and let it grow into a tree. In the `Shadows & Petals: Agriculture` tab you can also find `orange_seed` for growing `orange_tree` and harvesting `orange`.

## Furnishing a Room

Beyond the garden, the mod adds furnished interiors: the `irori` hearth opens a cooking container screen, lamp variants light your rooms, and `wind_chime`, `copper_teapot` and invisible `seat` entities decorate the space. Posts connect to posts, chains and hanging fittings to form support arms.
