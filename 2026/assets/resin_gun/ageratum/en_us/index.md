# Resin Gun

Resin Gun is a function-driven capture toolkit. Craft resin bullets and a resin gun, use projectiles to restrain mobs and leave resin pools, then seal supported mobs in portable amber according to their size class.

## Reading Route

1. [Crafting and Loading](01_crafting_and_loading): craft the gun and ammunition, then load one shot.
2. [Shooting and Resin Pools](02_shooting_and_resin_pools): understand projectiles, the sticky effect, and both pool sizes.
3. [Mob Sizes and Capture](03_mob_sizes_and_capture): prepare the correct conditions for small, medium, and large mobs.
4. [Amber and Release](04_amber_and_release): collect, move, and safely release captured mobs.
5. [Limits and Troubleshooting](05_limits_and_troubleshooting): check compatibility boundaries and current implementation caveats.

## Quick Reference

| Subject | Current Implementation |
|:---|:---|
| Core content | Resin Gun, Resin Bullet, Small Amber, Amber, and Large Amber |
| Survival recipes | 2 |
| Loading time | 3.5 seconds |
| Capture classes | Small, medium, and large |
| Config, commands, and keys | None |

::: info
These tools are implemented with component-bearing vanilla items and display entities. They are not ordinary registered `resin_gun:*` items. Obtain them through their recipes and do not build filters around the carrier item's underlying ID.
:::
