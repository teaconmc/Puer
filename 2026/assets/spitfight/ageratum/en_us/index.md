# Spit Fight

Spit Fight turns talking into a resource. Every chat message costs saliva, and the saliva you have left is also your ammunition: hold a key to charge, release to spit like a llama. The spit even carries your own status effects to whoever it hits — which means firing it takes those effects away from you.

Talk too much and you will be too dry to speak, let alone fight.
