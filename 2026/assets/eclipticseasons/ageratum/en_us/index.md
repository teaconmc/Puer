# Ecliptic Seasons

Ecliptic Seasons (`eclipticseasons`) runs the world on a full calendar of the 24 solar terms (节气). Each term carries its own temperature, rainfall, humidity and ecosystem mood, so crops, animals, weather and a growing set of "snowy" overlays all shift through the year. A toolkit of decorative, measuring and greenhouse blocks lets you read the season and push back against it.
